# Mercy in Revelation and Spirituality

## Summary

Rahma (divine mercy) is both a theological attribute (Ar-Rahman, Ar-Rahim) and a spiritual practice (showing mercy to creation). This synthesis connects the Quranic revelation of mercy with its cultivation in the soul (tazkiyah).

## Dimensions of Divine Mercy

### Theological Mercy

**Quranic basis:** [[quran-wiki/surah-001-al-fatihah|Al-Fatihah (1:1-3)]] — 'Bismillah ir-Rahman ir-Rahim... Ar-Rahman ir-Rahim' — the only divine attribute repeated twice in succession in the entire Qur'an.
**Meaning:** Ar-Rahman: mercy encompassing all creation (believers and disbelievers). Ar-Rahim: mercy specific to the believers in the Hereafter.

### Prophetic Mercy

**Quranic basis:** [[quran-wiki/surah-021-al-anbiya|Al-Anbiya (21:107)]] — 'We have not sent you except as a mercy to the worlds' — Muhammad (SAW) is the embodiment of mercy.
**Meaning:** The Prophet's character, compassion, and forgiveness are the practical model for how mercy operates in human relationships.

### Spiritual Practice

**Quranic basis:** [[quran-wiki/surah-007-al-a'raf|Al-A'raf (7:156)]] — 'My mercy encompasses all things. So I will decree it for those who fear Me and give zakah and those who believe in Our verses.'
**Meaning:** Mercy is conditional on taqwa and righteous action. The tazkiyah path cultivates these preconditions through dhikr, sabr, and purification of intention.

### Eschatological Mercy

**Quranic basis:** [[quran-wiki/surah-039-az-zumar|Az-Zumar (39:53)]] — 'Say: O My servants who have transgressed against themselves, do not despair of the mercy of Allah. Indeed Allah forgives all sins.'
**Meaning:** No sin is beyond forgiveness. This is the ultimate spiritual consolation and the foundation of tawbah (repentance) in tazkiyah.

## Synthesis

The Qur'an reveals mercy as **precedent** (it precedes wrath), **universal** (Ar-Rahman covers all creation), and **conditional** (Ar-Rahim for the muttaqin). Tazkiyah (future tazkiyah-wiki) is the discipline of aligning the soul with these conditions: taqwa, dhikr, sabr, and tawbah. The spiritual master (murshid) guides the murid through the stations (maqamat) that make divine mercy manifest in the heart.
