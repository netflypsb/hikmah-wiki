# The Five Pillars in the Qur'an

## Summary

The Five Pillars of Islam (arkan al-islam) are established primarily in the Qur'an and elaborated in the Sunnah and fiqh. This synthesis traces the Quranic basis (nas) for each pillar, with placeholders for future fiqh elaboration.

## The Five Pillars — Quranic Basis

### Shahada — Declaration of Faith

- **Quranic evidence:** [[quran-wiki/surah-003-aali-imran|Aali Imran (3:18)]] — 'Allah witnesses that there is no deity except Him, and the angels and those with knowledge...'
- **Key verse:** [[quran-wiki/surah-112-al-ikhlas|Al-Ikhlas (112)]] — The purest expression of tawhid: Qul huwa Allahu Ahad
- **Fiqh elaboration:** Fiqh elaboration: conditions of valid shahada, pronunciation, intention, apostasy rulings. (Awaiting fiqh-wiki.)

### Salah — Prayer

- **Quranic evidence:** [[quran-wiki/surah-020-ta-ha|Ta-Ha (20:14)]] — 'Indeed I am Allah. There is no deity except Me, so worship Me and establish prayer for My remembrance.'
- **Key verse:** [[quran-wiki/surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2:238)]] — 'Maintain with care the prescribed prayers and the middle prayer'
- **Fiqh elaboration:** Fiqh elaboration: times, rak'ahs, conditions, invalidators, makeup prayers, congregational rules. (Awaiting fiqh-wiki.)

### Zakat — Alms

- **Quranic evidence:** [[quran-wiki/surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2:110)]] — 'And establish prayer and give zakat, and whatever good you put forward for yourselves — you will find it with Allah.'
- **Key verse:** [[quran-wiki/surah-009-at-tawbah|At-Tawbah (9:60)]] — The eight categories of zakat recipients (asnaf) enumerated
- **Fiqh elaboration:** Fiqh elaboration: nisab rates, eligible assets, distribution rules, sadaqah vs zakat. (Awaiting fiqh-wiki.)

### Sawm — Fasting

- **Quranic evidence:** [[quran-wiki/surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2:183-185)]] — 'O you who have believed, decreed upon you is fasting as it was decreed upon those before you...'
- **Key verse:** [[quran-wiki/surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2:187)]] — Permitted during nights of Ramadan; complete fast until sunset
- **Fiqh elaboration:** Fiqh elaboration: moon sighting, exemptions, invalidators, qada and kaffarah, taraweeh. (Awaiting fiqh-wiki.)

### Hajj — Pilgrimage

- **Quranic evidence:** [[quran-wiki/surah-003-aali-imran|Aali Imran (3:97)]] — 'And Hajj to the House is a duty mankind owes to Allah, for those who are able to undertake the journey.'
- **Key verse:** [[quran-wiki/surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2:125, 2:196-203)]] — Ibrahim's station, the rituals (ihram, tawaf, sa'y, Arafat, Mash'ar, Mina)
- **Fiqh elaboration:** Fiqh elaboration: conditions of obligation, types (ifrad, qiran, tamattu'), prohibited acts in ihram, sacrifice rules. (Awaiting fiqh-wiki.)

## Synthesis

The Qur'an establishes the pillars as divine commands (amr) with both theological justification (tawhid, akhirah) and practical instruction. The fiqh elaboration — which the future fiqh-wiki will cover — operates within the framework of these Quranic commands, never contradicting them. Classical scholars (Ibn Kathir, al-Qurtubi) emphasized that the pillars are the 'foundation of the religion' (usul al-din) and their abandonment risks nullification of faith.
