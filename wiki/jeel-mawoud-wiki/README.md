> A comprehensive LLM Wiki built from the Arabic original

## About

This wiki is a structured knowledge base extracted from الدكتور مجدي الهلالي's book **الجيل الموعود بالنصر والتمكين** (*al-Jil al-Maw'ud bi an-Nasr wa at-Tamkin*), published by Dar al-Andalus al-Jadidah (Egypt, 2008, ISBN 977-6142-96-6).

The book identifies **10 attributes** that define the generation Allah promises victory and empowerment to, drawing from Qur'anic evidence, prophetic hadith, and sahabah exemplars.

## Key Concepts

- **النصر والتمكين** (an-Nasr wa at-Tamkin) — Victory & Empowerment: Allah's conditional promise
- **الأخذ بالأسباب** (al-Akhdhu bil Asbab) — Taking All Necessary Causes: the practical methodology
- **القاعدة الصلبة** (al-Qa'idah as-Salbah) — Solid Foundation: the base for building the generation
- **المعرفة بالله** (al-Ma'rifah billah) — Knowledge of Allah: the root of all attributes
- **التربية** (at-Tarbiyyah) — Education/Nurturing: the methodology of building the generation
- **تزكية النفس** (Tazkiyat an-Nafs) — Soul Purification: the internal transformation
