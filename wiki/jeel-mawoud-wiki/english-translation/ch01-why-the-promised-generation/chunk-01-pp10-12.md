**Chapter One**
**Why the Promised Generation?**

What is happening to our Islamic ummah — from humiliation (dhull) and weakness (duʿf) that is hidden from no one — wounds fill the body of the ummah, and weakness and frailty (wahn) have struck it. The Muslims are no longer united upon the heart of a single man, nor under a single banner; rather their banners have multiplied, their directions have diverged, and their enemies have pounced upon them from every direction.

We have become the most humiliated of the people of the earth. We have no value, and our existence carries no weight. It suffices [to consider] what is done to us by the brothers of apes and pigs and their allies, who have placed us under their feet and subjected us to the worst of torment. They kill our children, demolish our homes, and detain our youth. All of this happens under the sight and hearing of the entire world. A bitter and agonising state in which the ummah and Islam live — so until when will this continue?

Our hope is in Allah alone

--- PAGE 11 ---

What further complicates the matter is that the material and technological superiority which our enemies have attained makes it difficult for us to catch up with them, while at the same time reality tells us that they will not allow us to approach them, let alone surpass them. The space they have afforded us for movement is extremely limited.

This means that material calculations (ḥisābāt māddiyyah) confirm and establish the reality of the wretched condition which the Islamic ummah endures — nay, the situation will only worsen as a result of the boundless ambitions of global Zionism and global Crusaderism in the lands of Islam.

Yes, these are the calculations of the people of the earth (ḥisābāt ahl al-arḍ). As for the calculations of the people of faith (ḥisābāt ahl al-īmān), they tell us that there is a tremendous power, the magnitude of which the human mind cannot comprehend. This power, if it supports someone — no matter how weak he may be — then victory (naṣr) becomes his ally, regardless of the strength of his enemies.

It is the divine power (al-quwwah al-ilāhiyyah), the power of the Compeller (al-Jabbār) — Glorified and Exalted be He — who possesses the hosts of the heavens and the earth, who owns everything: He owns the winds and the storms, the thunder and the lightning, the earthquakes and the volcanoes, and… He owns the very breath of our enemies.

This power is our sole hope in confronting the assaults of our savage enemies, restoring our glory, and leading humanity once again.

This power is what supported the weak among the Children of Israel against the tyrant Pharaoh and his people. So what happened?

*And We caused the people who had been oppressed to inherit the eastern regions of the land and the western ones, which We had blessed. And the good word of your Lord was fulfilled for the Children of Israel because of what they had endured. And We destroyed what Pharaoh and his people had been building and what they had been raising.* (al-Aʿrāf 7:137)

**Shall We Abandon the Means?**

The meaning of saying "our hope is in Allah alone" is not that we abandon material means under the claim that they are ineffective, and that our enemies will not allow us to compete with them or approach them in fields of scientific progress. Rather, the requirement is the opposite: that we fill every available space, penetrate every sector, and exert the utmost effort in acquiring the means of power — not because we will achieve victory through them, but because Allah, Mighty and Majestic, has required this of us:

> ﴿وَأَعِدُّوا لَهُمْ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُمْ مِنْ قُوَّةٍ وَمِنْ رِبَاطِ الْخَيْلِ تُرْهِبُونَ بِهِ عَدُوَّ اللَّهِ وَعَدُوَّكُمْ﴾

*And prepare against them whatever you are able of power and of cavalry to strike terror into the enemy of Allah and your enemy.* (al-Anfāl 8:60)

And to the extent of our effort in taking advantage of the means available to us, we will have fulfilled a condition among the conditions of victory (naṣr) and change (taghyīr) — while bearing in mind that the means in and of themselves will not bring us victory. Rather, the one who will bring it is Allah. Is He not the One who says:

> ﴿وَمَا النَّصْرُ إِلَّا مِنْ عِنْدِ اللَّهِ﴾

*And victory is not but from Allah.* (al-Anfāl 8:10)

And the One who says:

> ﴿إِنْ يَنْصُرْكُمُ اللَّهُ فَلَا غَالِبَ لَكُمْ وَإِنْ يَخْذُلْكُمْ فَمَنْ ذَا الَّذِي يَنْصُرُكُمْ مِنْ بَعْدِهِ وَعَلَى اللَّهِ فَلْيَتَوَكَّلِ الْمُؤْمِنُونَ﴾

*If Allah supports you, none can overcome you; and if He forsakes you, who is it that can support you after Him? And upon Allah let the believers rely.* (Āl ʿImrān 3:160)

**Translator's notes for Chunk 1:**

1. **"Brothers of apes and pigs"** (إخوان القردة والخنازير): This is a reference to the Quranic account (5:60) of those whom Allah cursed and transformed. Al-Hilali uses this phrase to describe the enemies of the ummah. The phrase is retained as a faithful translation of the original; it reflects the author's rhetorical and theological framing, not a zoological claim.

2. **"Global Zionism and global Crusaderism"** (الصهيونية والصليبية العالمية): This is a common framing in Islamic revivalist literature, referring to what the author perceives as coordinated global ideological movements. "Crusaderism" (al-ṣalībiyyah) is the author's term for Western political-military intervention in Muslim lands, drawing on the historical memory of the Crusades.

3. **"The calculations of the people of the earth" vs. "the calculations of the people of faith"** (حسابات أهل الأرض / حسابات أهل الإيمان): This is the central conceptual contrast of pages 10–12. The author argues that material analysis alone yields despair, while faith-based analysis reveals divine power as the decisive factor.

4. **Key Arabic terms preserved:** dhull (humiliation), duʿf (weakness), wahn (frailty), naṣr (victory/victory), taghyīr (change), ḥisābāt ahl al-arḍ (calculations of the people of the earth), ḥisābāt ahl al-īmān (calculations of the people of faith), al-quwwah al-ilāhiyyah (divine power), al-Jabbār (the Compeller — one of Allah's names).
