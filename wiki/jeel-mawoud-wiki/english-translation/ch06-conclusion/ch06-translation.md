# Conclusion

And after all this,

Someone may say: What has been said in the preceding pages about the return of Islam’s glory anew, the return of the Caliphate, and the leadership of the world is merely a kind of fantasy and daydream, aimed at diverting attention from the bitter reality in which we live.

No, by Allah! Rather, these will be realities that we shall witness soon, and sooner than we expect, if we sincerely flee to Allah, return to His Book, and bind ourselves to it.

We shall see the might of Islam with our own eyes, if we all join hands in bringing about the *jīl al-mawʿūd* (الجيل الموعود), the generation promised victory and empowerment.

My Muslim brother, my Muslim sister:

The glory of Islam will certainly return anew — but when?

This question’s answer depends on me and on you. By our own hands, if we become among the *jīl al-taghayyur* (جيل التغيير), the generation of change, we will answer this question; for the help of Allah is near:

> “Unquestionably, the help of Allah is near.” [Qur’an, Al-Baqarah 2:214]

This victory awaits the one who deserves it. Do not ask about the manner in which victory and empowerment will occur, for that is not our work; it is the work of Allah, the Mighty and Majestic.

Indeed, the One who brought Joseph out of prison and raised him to the throne through a dream that the king saw and Joseph interpreted — peace be upon him — is able to grant us victory in ways we cannot imagine. And the One who granted victory to Muhammad, peace and blessings be upon him, and returned him to Makkah as a conquering victor after he had left it driven out, is able to grant us victory and restore our glory and might:

> “Do you not know that Allah is over all things competent? Do you not know that to Allah belongs the dominion of the heavens and the earth?” [Qur’an, Al-Baqarah 2:106–107]

We ask Allah, the Mighty and Majestic, to make us, our spouses, and our children among the generation of change, those whom He loves and who love Him; to use us and not replace us; and we likewise ask Him to grant us our hopes, forgive our sins, and make our end good.

And all praise is due to Allah, Who guided us to this; we would not have been guided had Allah not guided us.

O Allah, send prayers and peace upon our master Muhammad, and upon his family and companions, and grant him peace.

And all praise is due to Allah, Lord of the Worlds.
