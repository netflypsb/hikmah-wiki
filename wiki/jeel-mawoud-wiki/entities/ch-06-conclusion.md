# Conclusion (الخاتمة)

## Summary

The conclusion of the book is a brief but powerful call to action. The author anticipates the objection that the return of Islam's glory, the khilāfah, and civilisational leadership are mere dreams and fantasies meant to distract from bitter reality. He emphatically denies this: these will become realities we may witness soon — and sooner than expected — if we properly flee to Allah, return to His Book, and bind ourselves to it. He declares that we will see the glory of Islam with our own eyes if we cooperate in bringing about the promised generation. The question of "when?" is answered by the reader's own response: it is in our hands — if we become the generation of change, Allah's naṣr is near. The author cites {أَلَا إِنَّ نَصْرَ اللَّهِ قَرِيبٌ} (2:214) and reminds us that the One who brought Yūsuf from prison to the throne, and who returned Muḥammad ﷺ to Makkah as a conqueror after being driven out, is fully capable of granting us victory and restoring our glory — {أَلَمْ تَعْلَمْ أَنَّ اللَّهَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ ۝ أَلَمْ تَعْلَمْ أَنَّ اللَّهَ لَهُ مُلْكُ السَّمَاوَاتِ وَالْأَرْضِ} (2:106–107). The book closes with a supplication that Allah make us, our spouses, and our children among the generation of change whom He loves and who love Him, that He employ us and not replace us, and that He grant us our hopes, forgive our sins, and grant us a good ending.

## Key Arguments

- The promise of Islam's return to glory is not a dream or fantasy but a coming reality — conditioned on our fleeing to Allah and returning to His Book (p. 192)
- We will witness Islam's glory with our own eyes if we cooperate to produce the promised generation (p. 192)
- The question "when will Islam return?" is answered by our own response: if we become the generation of change, naṣr is near (p. 192)
- The same Allah who elevated Yūsuf from prison to the throne and returned Muḥammad ﷺ to Makkah as a conqueror is capable of granting us victory (pp. 192–193)
- The conclusion is an appeal to personal responsibility and divine omnipotence combined — the conditions are ours to fulfil, the victory is Allah's to grant (p. 193)
