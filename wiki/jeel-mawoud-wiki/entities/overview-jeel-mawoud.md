# الجيل الموعود بالنصر والتمكين — Book Overview

## Overview

الجيل الموعود بالنصر والتمكين (*al-Jil al-Maw'ud bi an-Nasr wa at-Tamkin* — "The Generation Promised Victory and Empowerment") is a foundational tarbiyyah work by [[entities/scholar-majdi-al-hilali|Dr. Majdi al-Hilali]] that addresses one of the most urgent questions facing the Muslim ummah: how does Allah's promise of victory (*nasr*, نصر) and empowerment (*tamkin*, تمكين) become reality? The book's central thesis is that Allah has promised a group of His believing servants that they will inherit the earth and be established in it — as stated in Qur'an 7:128 and 24:55 — but He did not specify a time or place for the appearance of this victorious group. Instead, He specified **conditions and attributes** (*shurut wa sifat*): whenever a generation fulfils these attributes, in any time or place, Allah's promise will be realised for them. The book identifies **10 core Qur'anic attributes** that this promised generation must embody. Drawing on the historical proof of the first generation — the Sahabah (رضوان الله عليهم), who fulfilled these conditions and received swift, generous divine support — the author argues that the ummah's current state of weakness, humiliation, and fragmentation will only end when a new generation arises that completes these same attributes. The work is both diagnostic (identifying the ummah's ailment) and prescriptive (charting the path of spiritual and practical change that leads to divine victory).

## Central Thesis

The promise of Allah — victory and empowerment for the believers — is **conditional**, not unconditional. Qur'an 24:55 makes this explicit: *وَعَدَ اللَّهُ الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا مِنكُمْ وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ لَيَسْتَخْلِفَنَّهُمْ فِي الْأَرْضِ* ("Allah has promised those who believe among you and do righteous deeds that He will surely grant them succession in the earth"). The conditions are iman (faith) and 'amal salih (righteous action) — embodied concretely in 10 Qur'anic attributes detailed in Chapter 2. As the author states in the introduction: Allah did not fix a time or place for the appearance of the victorious group; rather, He fixed conditions and attributes — whenever a generation fulfils them, His promise will be realised. This means that mere activism, political effort, or material preparation — while necessary — are insufficient without the inner transformation that produces a generation matching the Qur'anic blueprint. The Sahabah proved this: they fulfilled the conditions and received divine victory within a few years. The book's mission is to identify those attributes, explain how they are cultivated, and assess where the contemporary ummah stands in relation to them.

## 10 Core Attributes

The book identifies ten Qur'anic attributes that the promised generation must embody (detailed in Chapter 2, pp. 24–97):

1. **الإخلاص** (*al-Ikhlas*) — Sincerity to Allah → [[concepts/concept-ikhlas|Ikhlas]]
2. **محبة الله** (*Mahabbatullah*) — Love of Allah → [[concepts/concept-mahabbah|Mahabbah]]
3. **الخوف من الله** (*Khawfullah*) — Fear of Allah → [[concepts/concept-khawf|Khawf]]
4. **العبادة** (*al-Ibadah*) — Worship ("Monks of the Night") → [[concepts/concept-ibadah|Ibadah]]
5. **التواضع** (*at-Tawadu'*) — Humility → [[concepts/concept-tawadu|Tawadu']]
6. **الزهد** (*az-Zuhd*) — Asceticism / Worldly Detachment → [[concepts/concept-zuhd|Zuhd]]
7. **الجهاد** (*al-Jihad*) — Striving / Struggle → [[concepts/concept-jihad|Jihad]]
8. **الصبر والثبات** (*as-Sabr wa ath-Thabat*) — Patience & Steadfastness → [[concepts/concept-sabr|Sabr]]
9. **الاعتدال والتوازن** (*al-I'tidal wa at-Tawazun*) — Moderation & Balance → [[concepts/concept-itidal|I'tidal]]
10. **الترابط والأخوة** (*at-Tarabut wa al-Ukhuwwah*) — Cohesion & Brotherhood → [[concepts/concept-ukhuwwah|Ukhuwwah]]
