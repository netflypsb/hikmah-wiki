# Aqidah (The Islamic Creed)

Aqidah — the Islamic creed — is the foundation of a Muslim's worldview and the starting point of the path outlined in [[fathi-yakan]]'s *To Be a Muslim*. Section 1.1 presents the aqidah as five interconnected beliefs that shape a believer's knowledge, action, and destiny.

## 1. Belief in Allah (Tawhid)

Allah is the Creator, the All-Knowing, the Almighty — utterly unique and without partner. This is *tawhid*: the absolute oneness of God. The Qur'an insists that if there were other gods besides Allah, the heavens and earth would fall into corruption (21:22). The purpose of human existence flows directly from tawhid: *"I created jinn and mankind only to worship Me"* (51:56–58) — to know, serve, and worship Allah. Allah is known through His 99 attributes (*asma' al-husna*), each a doorway to deeper understanding of the divine nature.

## 2. Belief in the Final Judgement (Akhirah)

Life ends before an impartial Judge. Believers receive Paradise; disbelievers receive hell-fire (42:7). This belief is no denial of human freedom — people choose, and Allah grants *taufiq* (divine guidance) to those who seek it (7:10). Every soul is accountable for its own deeds: *"no soul shall bear the burden of another"* (74:38). Yet Allah is never distant — *"He is with you wherever you are"* (58:7).

## 3. Belief in Revelation

Allah communicates through Prophets and revealed Books. Muhammad is the final prophet and the Qur'an is his eternal miracle — inimitable, preserved, and complete. The *shari'ah* (divine law) cannot be overruled; Muslims are limited to *ijtehad* (reasoned interpretation) within its bounds. Human traditions (*sunnah*) and authoritative interpretation (*ta'wil*) are essential for understanding revelation in every age.

## 4. Belief in Charity (Mahabbah)

True aqidah produces *mahabbah* — love of Allah that overflows into good deeds and self-sacrifice. The Prophet said: *"There are three qualities, whoever has them finds the sweetness of faith: that Allah and His Messenger are dearer to him than anything else…"* (Bukhari). This love is uncompromising: *"Say: If your fathers, your sons, your brothers, your spouses, your tribe, the wealth you have gained… are dearer to you than Allah and His Messenger… then wait until Allah brings His decision"* (9:24). The creed without charity is a creed without taste.

## 5. Belief in Prayer (Ibadah)

Worship belongs to Allah alone (16:36). Prayer is the heartbeat of aqidah, expressed through:
- **Learning** the 99 names — each name a form of devotion.
- **Dhikr** (remembrance): *"Those who believe and whose hearts find rest in the remembrance of Allah"* (13:28); neglect it and Allah withdraws closeness (43:36).
- **Pondering creation**, not the divine essence (*dhat*) — the finite cannot grasp the infinite.
- **Fearing only Allah**: *"Those who fear their Lord in secret, for them is forgiveness and a great reward"* (67:12).
- **Tawakkul** (trust): *"Whoever puts their trust in Allah, He is sufficient for them"* (65:3).
- **Thankfulness**: Gratitude for every blessing (16:78) — *"If you are grateful, I will give you more"* (14:7).
- **Istighfar** (repentance): *"Whoever does evil or wrongs their own soul, then seeks forgiveness from Allah, will find Allah Forgiving, Merciful"* (4:110; cf. 3:135–6).
