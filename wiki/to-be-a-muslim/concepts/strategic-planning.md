# Strategic Planning (Section 2.6)

The Islamic movement must be clear in its purpose, goals, and strategies,
distinguishing them from subordinate objectives and tactics. Clarity of
purpose is a source of strength for the fully committed.

## Clear Goals

The purpose of Revelation is for mankind to serve Allah individually and
collectively (Qur'an 51:56). Muslims are servants of Allah in all matters
— prayer, business, governance, judgment — not only in religious affairs.
Islam rejects secularism's separation of religion and state. This servitude
means rejecting all man-made philosophies that lead mankind to submit to
the false gods of materialism. Two reasons: (1) they transgress Allah's
rights (Qur'an 6:57) and (2) they are inherently weak, unable to bring out
man's true nature (Qur'an 16:17).

## Clear Methods

The goal is to replace un-Islamic systems with a total Islamic one. Every
effort must serve this overriding goal. The Prophet's path shows four
critical methodological requirements:

### 1. Comprehensive

Movement work must include four elements: implementation of thought and
political strategy, organisation and tactical planning, human preparation,
and manpower plus weapons. These are complementary (Qur'an 8:39).

### 2. Universal

Active everywhere, in every country and society. Islam is universal in
thought and governance. Local problems have global links. Duties extend
beyond solving symptoms — the movement must expose the root causes within
un-Islamic systems. Example: inflation is identified as a natural result
of monopoly capitalism; the Islamic solution requires broad-based ownership
of the means of production.

### 3. Faithful to the Way of the Prophet

Four principles from the Prophet's life-example:
- Declaration of submission to Allah alone
- Formation of a committed group bound by faith (Dar al-Arqam, Badr, Qadisiyyah)
- Carefully prepared all-out effort against jahiliyyah
- Material strength balanced with spiritual strength

### 4. Materially Strong

A successful movement requires balance between material and non-material
strength. Neglect of either leads to extremism. [[hasan-al-banna]] explained
the role of strength: Qur'an 8:60 commands preparing strength to the utmost.
The Prophet said: "A strong believer is better and more loved by Allah than
the weak believer." Strength hierarchy: faith > togetherness > manpower >
weapons. No group can be strong without values.

The question of when force is justified is left as a strategic decision for
each stage. See [[islamic-movement]], [[baiah]], [[islamic-activism]], and
[[fathi-yakan]].
