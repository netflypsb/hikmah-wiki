# Brotherhood (Section 2.7.6)

In Islam, faith (aqidah) is the core of brotherhood. It keeps Muslims close
to each other in a fraternal relationship based on each member's submission
to Allah and their mutual efforts to draw nearer to Him.

## The Bond of Faith

"The strongest relationship is built on loving for the sake of Allah and
becoming angry for the sake of Allah." (Ahmad) This bond strengthens the
process of building a Muslim community. An Islamic movement based on true
brotherhood is as solid as concrete and functions with organic unity — a
body that feels pain when any part suffers.

## Six Rights of Brotherhood

The Prophet (s) specified six duties of a Muslim to another Muslim
(Narrated by Muslim):

1. Say salaam when you meet
2. Accept invitations
3. Give sincere advice when asked
4. Say "Yarhamukallah" when a brother sneezes and says "Alhamdulillah"
5. Visit the sick
6. Accompany the deceased to the cemetery

## Practical Dimensions

- **Spiritual help** — "Those whom Allah wishes to be given good deeds,
  Allah will sustain them by a pious Muslim friend." If he forgets, his
  friend reminds him. 'Umar al-Khattab: "Seek out trustworthy brothers and
  live in their community, because they are the embellishers during
  happiness and suppliers in hardship."

- **Material cooperation** — "Those who help a Muslim in hardship in this
  world will be protected by Allah from suffering hardship in the
  Hereafter." (Muslim, Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Nasa'i, Ibn Majah)

- **Caring and love** — "Do not end a friendship, do not turn your back,
  do not hate each other, and do not envy each other. Two Muslims may not
  remain on non-speaking terms for more than three days." (Malik, Bukhari,
  Abu Dawud, Tirmidhi, Nasa'i)

- **Generosity** — "Exchange gifts, so that you will love each other and
  the enmity between you will vanish." (Bukhari & Muslim)

- **Loyalty** — "If one defends the honour of his fellow Muslim, Allah
  will keep the Hellfire away from his face in the Hereafter." (Tirmidhi)

- **Supplication** — "The supplication of a Muslim for his brother without
  his knowledge is an accepted supplication." (Muslim) An angel says:
  "Amen, and the same for you too."
