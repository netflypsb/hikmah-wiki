# Islamic Activism (Section 2.2)

Working for Islam means reforming oneself so that one's life teaches true
belief and Islamic behaviour, and forming a society committed to the Islamic
way of thinking and living. This obligation is compulsory for every
individual Muslim — not merely a collective duty. Sins of omission will not
be forgiven until action is taken. The obligation operates on five levels.

## In Principle

Working for Islam is the core of all duties prescribed by Allah. All
prophets and messengers enjoined it. Qur'an 103:1-3 swears by time that
man is in loss except those who believe, do good, and counsel one another
in truth and steadfastness. Concealing guidance brings a curse (Qur'an
2:159). The Prophet said: "Whoever of you sees an evil action, let him
change it with his hand; if he cannot, then with his tongue; if he cannot,
then with his heart — and that is the weakest of faith." (Tirmidhi)

## By Law (Shari'ah)

When existing systems deny the wisdom of Allah, it becomes compulsory to
establish functionally Islamic governance. Qur'an 4:65 demands that
believers refer all disputes to Allah's judgment. Qur'an 5:44-47 declares
that failing to judge by Allah's revelation makes one an unbeliever,
wrongdoer, or rebel. Whenever an obligatory goal cannot be achieved without
a prerequisite, that prerequisite becomes obligatory.

## In Self-Defense

Many Muslim communities face oppression from non-believing occupiers
(Palestine, Kashmir, Eritrea, and others) or brutal minorities. Forming
Islamic opposition is a fiqhi obligation: "Fight them until there is no
more tumult or oppression, and there prevail justice and faith in Allah."
(Qur'an 8:39)

## In Community

Every Muslim bears personal responsibility (Qur'an 74:38, 6:164, 29:6),
but the work is too great for individuals alone. An organised movement is
necessary — the Prophet's own example proves this. He did not operate
alone but formed a community from the start. "Help one another in
righteousness and piety." (Qur'an 5:2)

## In Jihad

Those who work for Islam need Islam more than anyone. The path is long
and full of hardship, but constancy is a condition of steadfastness.
"A wolf eats only a sheep separated from the herd." Those who remain in
isolation risk Hellfire. The committed join an honourable people — the
prophets and martyrs. See [[islamic-movement]] and [[baiah]] for the
organisational framework that sustains activism. This activism is the
practical outworking of [[aqidah]], driven by [[ibadah]], and sustained
by [[self-control]].
