# Bai'ah and the Requirements of Brotherhood (Sections 2.5, 2.7)

Bai'ah is the oath of loyalty that binds a Muslim to the movement's leader
and ummah. It is a prophetic tradition — the first and second bai'at
al-'Aqabah and the bai'at ar-ridwan demonstrate its practice. To make
bai'ah is to submit one's entire life to the leader and community in agreed
matters, whether one likes it or not. Its violation is a sin (ma'siya).

## Quality Over Quantity

The movement prioritises quality. Uncommitted or counterproductive
members threaten survival. The Battle of Hunain showed that large numbers
do not guarantee victory. Each member is evaluated and given a developmental
programme tailored to their strengths.

## The Ten Principles of Bai'ah

Imam [[hasan-al-banna]] defined ten principles:

1. **Understanding** — The ideology is purely Islamic. Twenty sub-principles
   cover: Islam as a complete system; Qur'an and sunnah as ultimate sources;
   rejection of mystical claims contrary to shari'ah; prohibition of
   talismans and fortune-telling; maslaha mursalah acceptable within
   shari'ah bounds; following one of four madhhabs for the unlearned;
   no disunity over minor differences; avoiding theoretical issues with
   no practical benefit; tawhid as the core of creed; accepting divine
   attributes without distortion; and more.

2. **Sincerity** — Aspire only to please Allah. No self-interest, prestige,
   or title-seeking.

3. **Ibadah (Worship Through Action)** — Perfect oneself, build a Muslim
   family, educate the community, liberate the nation, correct the ruler,
   restore the ummah's influence, and propagate Islam to the ends of the
   earth.

4. **Jihad** — Continuous struggle until the Day of Judgement. Not engaging
   in jihad or having no intention for it is jahiliyyah.

5. **Sacrifice** — Sacrifice life, wealth, time, everything for the goal.

6. **Obedience** — Obey every command, whether in happiness or hardship,
   liked or disliked.

7. **Perseverance** — Worship Allah and wage jihad until death. The goal
   may take a lifetime.

8. **Integrity** — Be true to yourself, honest in thinking. Refuse to
   compromise with falsehood. "The baptism of Allah — who can baptise
   better than Allah?"

9. **Brotherhood** — Unite soul and spirit with faith and belief.

10. **Trust** — Confidence in leadership's competence, sincerity, and
    ability inspires love, honour, and obedience.

## The Thirty-Plus Responsibilities

Hasan al-Banna's Educational Discourse (Risalat Ta'alim) lists over 30
personal obligations: daily Qur'an recitation (at least 1 juz), studying
sirah, memorising 40 hadith from Imam al-Nawawi, studying aqidah and fiqh,
health care, cleanliness, truthfulness, bravery, modesty, fairness, active
community service, economic self-sufficiency, avoiding riba and gambling,
supporting da'wah financially, maintaining Islamic culture, and constant
dhikr and night prayer.

## Brotherhood

Brotherhood in Islam is a bond of faith — "the strongest relationship is
built on loving for the sake of Allah" (Ahmad). It has six duties (Muslim):
greeting, accepting invitations, advising, responding to sneezes, visiting
the sick, and accompanying the deceased. Brotherhood is both spiritual bond
and practical cooperation: helping a brother fulfil his duty is better than
two months of i'tikaf in the Prophet's mosque (al-Hakim). See
[[islamic-movement]], [[hasan-al-banna]], [[fathi-yakan]].
