# القدر — Al-Qadr (97)

> Al-Qadr — 5 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Al-Qadr (القدر) is the 97th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **5 verses**, spanning **juz 30** (pages 598–598 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **1 rukus**.

## Tafsir Summary

### Ayah 1

**Ibn Kathir:** The Virtues of the Night of Al-Qadr (the Decree) Allah informs that He sent the Qur'an down during the Night of Al-Qadr, and it is a blessed night about which Allah says, إِنَّآ أَنزَلْنَـهُ فِى لَيْلَةٍ مُّبَـرَكَةٍ (We sent it down on a blessed night.) (44:3) This is the Night of Al-Qadr and it occurs during the month of Ramadan. This is as Allah says, شَهْرُ رَمَضَانَ الَّذِى أُنزِلَ فِيهِ …

**Muyassar:** إنا أنزلنا القرآن في ليلة الشرف والفضل، وهي إحدى ليالي شهر رمضان.

### Ayah 2

**Ibn Kathir:** The Virtues of the Night of Al-Qadr (the Decree) Allah informs that He sent the Qur'an down during the Night of Al-Qadr, and it is a blessed night about which Allah says, إِنَّآ أَنزَلْنَـهُ فِى لَيْلَةٍ مُّبَـرَكَةٍ (We sent it down on a blessed night.) (44:3) This is the Night of Al-Qadr and it occurs during the month of Ramadan. This is as Allah says, شَهْرُ رَمَضَانَ الَّذِى أُنزِلَ فِيهِ …

**Muyassar:** وما أدراك -أيها النبي- ما ليلة القدر والشرف؟

### Ayah 3

**Ibn Kathir:** The Virtues of the Night of Al-Qadr (the Decree) Allah informs that He sent the Qur'an down during the Night of Al-Qadr, and it is a blessed night about which Allah says, إِنَّآ أَنزَلْنَـهُ فِى لَيْلَةٍ مُّبَـرَكَةٍ (We sent it down on a blessed night.) (44:3) This is the Night of Al-Qadr and it occurs during the month of Ramadan. This is as Allah says, شَهْرُ رَمَضَانَ الَّذِى أُنزِلَ فِيهِ …

**Muyassar:** ليلة القدر ليلة مباركة، العمل الصالح فيها خير من عمل ألف شهر ليس فيها ليلة قدر.
