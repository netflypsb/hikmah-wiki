# غافر — Ghafir (40)

> Ghafir — 85 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Ghafir (غافر) is the 40th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **85 verses**, spanning **juz 24** (pages 467–476 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **9 rukus**.
