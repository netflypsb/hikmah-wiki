# الفجر — Al-Fajr (89)

> Al-Fajr — 30 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Al-Fajr (الفجر) is the 89th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **30 verses**, spanning **juz 30** (pages 593–594 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **1 rukus**.

## Tafsir Summary

### Ayah 1

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Makkah Recitation of Surat Al-Fajr in the Prayer An-Nasa'i recorded a narration from Jabir that Mu`adh prayed a prayer and a man came and joined him in the prayer. Mu`adh made the prayer long, so the man went and prayed (alone) at the side of the Masjid, and then left. When Mu`adh was informed of this he said, "(He is) a hypocrite." He (Mu`adh) then informed the Messenger of …

**Muyassar:** أقسم الله سبحانه بوقت الفجر، والليالي العشر الأوَل من ذي الحجة وما شرفت به، وبكل شفع وفرد، وبالليل إذا يَسْري بظلامه، أليس في الأقسام المذكورة مَقْنَع لذي عقل؟
