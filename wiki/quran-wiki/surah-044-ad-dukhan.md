# الدخان — Ad-Dukhan (44)

> Ad-Dukhan — 59 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Ad-Dukhan (الدخان) is the 44th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **59 verses**, spanning **juz 25** (pages 496–498 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **3 rukus**.

## Tafsir Summary

### Ayah 1

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Makkah In Musnad Al-Bazzar, it is recorded from Abu At-Tufayl `Amir bin Wathilah from Zayd bin Harithah that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ said to Ibn Sayyad: «إِنِّي قَدْ خَبَأْتُ خَبْأً فَمَا هُوَ؟» (I am concealing something, what is it) And the Messenger of Allah ﷺ was concealing Surat Ad-Dukhan from him. He (Ibn Sayyad) said: "It is Ad-Dukh." The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said, …

**Muyassar:** {حم} سبق الكلام على الحروف المقطَّعة في أول سورة البقرة.
