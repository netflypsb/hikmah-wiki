# طه — Taha (20)

> Taha — 135 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Taha (طه) is the 20th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **135 verses**, spanning **juz 16** (pages 312–321 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **8 rukus**.
