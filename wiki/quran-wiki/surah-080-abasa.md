# عبس — Abasa (80)

> Abasa — 42 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Abasa (عبس) is the 80th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **42 verses**, spanning **juz 30** (pages 585–585 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **1 rukus**.
