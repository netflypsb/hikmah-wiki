# يوسف — Yusuf (12)

> Yusuf — 111 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Yusuf (يوسف) is the 12th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **111 verses**, spanning **juz 12, 13** (pages 235–248 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **12 rukus**.

## Tafsir Summary

### Ayah 1

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Makkah بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَـنِ الرَّحِيمِ In the Name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Qualities of the Qur'an In the beginning of Surat Al-Baqarah we talked about the separate letters, Allah said, تِلْكَ ءايَـتُ الْكِتَـبِ (These are the verses of the Book) in reference to the Clear Qur'an that is plain and apparent, and explains, clarifies and makes …

**Muyassar:** (الر) سبق الكلام على الحروف المقطَّعة في أول سورة البقرة. هذه آيات الكتاب البيِّن الواضح في معانيه وحلاله وحرامه وهداه.

### Ayah 2

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Makkah بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَـنِ الرَّحِيمِ In the Name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Qualities of the Qur'an In the beginning of Surat Al-Baqarah we talked about the separate letters, Allah said, تِلْكَ ءايَـتُ الْكِتَـبِ (These are the verses of the Book) in reference to the Clear Qur'an that is plain and apparent, and explains, clarifies and makes …

**Muyassar:** إنا أنزلنا هذا القرآن بلغة العرب، لعلكم -أيها العرب- تعقلون معانيه وتفهمونها، وتعملون بهديه.

### Ayah 3

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Makkah بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَـنِ الرَّحِيمِ In the Name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Qualities of the Qur'an In the beginning of Surat Al-Baqarah we talked about the separate letters, Allah said, تِلْكَ ءايَـتُ الْكِتَـبِ (These are the verses of the Book) in reference to the Clear Qur'an that is plain and apparent, and explains, clarifies and makes …

**Muyassar:** نحن نقصُّ عليك -أيها الرسول- أحسن القصص بوحينا إليك هذا القرآن، وإن كنت قبل إنزاله عليك لمن الغافلين عن هذه الأخبار، لا تدري عنها شيئًا.
