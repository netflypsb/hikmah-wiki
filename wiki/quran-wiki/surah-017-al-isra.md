# الإسراء — Al-Isra (17)

> Al-Isra — 111 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Al-Isra (الإسراء) is the 17th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **111 verses**, spanning **juz 15** (pages 282–293 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **12 rukus**.

### Ayah 2

**Ibn Kathir:** Musa and how He was given the Tawrah When Allah mentions how He took His servant Muhammad ﷺ, on the Journey by Night, He follows it by mentioning Musa, His servant and Messenger who also spoke with Him. Allah often mentions Muhammad ﷺ and Musa (AH.) together, may the peace and blessings of Allah be upon them both, and he mentions the Tawrah and the Qur'an together. So after mentioning the Isra', …

**Muyassar:** وكما كرَّم الله محمدًا صلى الله عليه وسلم بالإسراء، كَرَّم موسى عليه السلام بإعطائه التوراة، وجعلها بيانًا للحق وإرشادًا لبني إسرائيل، متضمنة نهيهم عن اتخاذ غير الله تعالى وليًا أو معبودًا يفوضون إليه أمورهم.
