# الغاشية — Al-Ghashiyah (88)

> Al-Ghashiyah — 26 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Al-Ghashiyah (الغاشية) is the 88th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **26 verses**, spanning **juz 30** (pages 592–593 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **1 rukus**.

## Tafsir Summary

### Ayah 1

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Makkah Reciting Surat Al-A`la and Al-Ghashiyah in the Friday Prayer It has already been mentioned on the authority of An-Nu`man bin Bashir that the Messenger of Allah ﷺ used to recite Surat Al-A`la (87) and Al-Ghashiyah in the `Id and Friday prayers. Imam Malik recorded that Ad-Dahhak bin Qays asked An-Nu`man bin Bashir, "What else did the Messenger of Allah ﷺ recite on …

**Muyassar:** هل أتاك -أيها الرسول- خبر القيامة التي تغشى الناس بأهوالها؟
