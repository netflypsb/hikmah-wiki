# الكافرون — Al-Kafirun (109)

> Al-Kafirun — 6 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Al-Kafirun (الكافرون) is the 109th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **6 verses**, spanning **juz 30** (pages 603–603 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **1 rukus**.
