# الرحمن — Ar-Rahman (55)

> Ar-Rahman — 78 verses, Medinan

## Overview

Ar-Rahman (الرحمن) is the 55th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Medinan** surah comprising **78 verses**, spanning **juz 27** (pages 531–534 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **3 rukus**.
