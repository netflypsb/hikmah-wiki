# قريش — Quraysh (106)

> Quraysh — 4 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Quraysh (قريش) is the 106th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **4 verses**, spanning **juz 30** (pages 602–602 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **1 rukus**.
