# التكاثر — At-Takathur (102)

> At-Takathur — 8 verses, Meccan

## Overview

At-Takathur (التكاثر) is the 102th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **8 verses**, spanning **juz 30** (pages 600–600 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **1 rukus**.

## Tafsir Summary

### Ayah 1

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Makkah بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَـنِ الرَّحِيمِ In the Name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. The Result of Loving the World and Heedlessness of the Hereafter Allah says that all are preoccupied by love of the world, its delights and its adornments, and this distracts you from seeking the Hereafter and desiring it. This delays you until death comes to you and you …

**Muyassar:** شغلكم عن طاعة الله التفاخر بكثرة الأموال والأولاد.

### Ayah 2

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Makkah بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَـنِ الرَّحِيمِ In the Name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. The Result of Loving the World and Heedlessness of the Hereafter Allah says that all are preoccupied by love of the world, its delights and its adornments, and this distracts you from seeking the Hereafter and desiring it. This delays you until death comes to you and you …

**Muyassar:** واستمر اشتغالكم بذلك إلى أن صرتم إلى المقابر، ودُفنتم فيها.

### Ayah 3

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Makkah بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَـنِ الرَّحِيمِ In the Name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. The Result of Loving the World and Heedlessness of the Hereafter Allah says that all are preoccupied by love of the world, its delights and its adornments, and this distracts you from seeking the Hereafter and desiring it. This delays you until death comes to you and you …

**Muyassar:** ما هكذا ينبغي أن يُلْهيكم التكاثر بالأموال، سوف تتبيَّنون أن الدار الآخرة خير لكم.
