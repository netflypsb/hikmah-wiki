# الفلق — Al-Falaq (113)

> Al-Falaq — 5 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Al-Falaq (الفلق) is the 113th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **5 verses**, spanning **juz 30** (pages 604–604 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **1 rukus**.
