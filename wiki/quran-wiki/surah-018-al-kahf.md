# الكهف — Al-Kahf (18)

> Al-Kahf — 110 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Al-Kahf (الكهف) is the 18th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **110 verses**, spanning **juz 15, 16** (pages 293–304 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **12 rukus**.

## Tafsir Summary

### Ayah 1

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Makkah What has been mentioned about the Virtues of this Surah and the first and last ten Ayat Imam Ahmad recorded that Al-Bara' said: "A man recited Al-Kahf and there was an animal in the house which began acting in a nervous manner. He looked, and saw a fog or cloud overhead. He mentioned this to the Prophet , who said: «اقْرَأْ فُلَانُ، فَإِنَّهَا السَّكِينَةُ تَنْزِلُ …

**Muyassar:** الثناء على الله بصفاته التي كلُّها أوصاف كمال، وبنعمه الظاهرة والباطنة، الدينية والدنيوية، الذي تفضَّل فأنزل على عبده ورسوله محمد صلى الله عليه وسلم القرآن، ولم يجعل فيه شيئًا من الميل عن الحق.
