# التكوير — At-Takwir (81)

> At-Takwir — 29 verses, Meccan

## Overview

At-Takwir (التكوير) is the 81th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **29 verses**, spanning **juz 30** (pages 586–586 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **1 rukus**.
