# يس — Ya-Sin (36)

> Ya-Sin — 83 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Ya-Sin (يس) is the 36th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **83 verses**, spanning **juz 22, 23** (pages 440–445 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **5 rukus**.

## Tafsir Summary

### Ayah 1

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Makkah The Virtues of Surah Ya Sin Al-Hafiz Abu Ya`la recorded that Abu Hurayrah, may Allah be pleased with him, said, "The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said: «مَنْ قَرَأَ يس فِي لَيْلَةٍ أَصْبَحَ مَغْفُورًا لَهُ، وَمَنْ قَرَأَ حم الَّتِي يُذْكَرُ فِيهَا الدُّخَانُ أَصْبَحَ مَغْفُورًا لَه» (Whoever recites Ya Sin in the night, will wake up forgiven, and whoever recites Ha Mim in …

**Muyassar:** {يس} سبق الكلام على الحروف المقطَّعة في أول سورة البقرة.
