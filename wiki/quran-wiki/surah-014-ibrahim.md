# إبراهيم — Ibrahim (14)

> Ibrahim — 52 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Ibrahim (إبراهيم) is the 14th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **52 verses**, spanning **juz 13** (pages 255–261 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **7 rukus**.
