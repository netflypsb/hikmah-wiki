# الروم — Ar-Rum (30)

> Ar-Rum — 60 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Ar-Rum (الروم) is the 30th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **60 verses**, spanning **juz 21** (pages 404–410 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **6 rukus**.
