# النبأ — An-Naba (78)

> An-Naba — 40 verses, Meccan

## Overview

An-Naba (النبأ) is the 78th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **40 verses**, spanning **juz 30** (pages 582–583 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **2 rukus**.
