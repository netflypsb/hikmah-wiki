# العنكبوت — Al-Ankabut (29)

> Al-Ankabut — 69 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Al-Ankabut (العنكبوت) is the 29th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **69 verses**, spanning **juz 20, 21** (pages 396–404 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **7 rukus**.

## Tafsir Summary

### Ayah 1

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Makkah بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَـنِ الرَّحِيمِ In the Name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. The Believers are tested so that it may be known Who is Sincere and Who is Lying Allah says: الم Alif Lam Mim. In the beginning of the Tafsir of Surah Al-Baqarah, we discussed the letters which appear at the beginning of some Surahs. أَحَسِبَ النَّاسُ أَن يُتْرَكُواْ أَن …

**Muyassar:** {الم} سبق الكلام على الحروف المقطَّعة في أول سورة البقرة.

### Ayah 2

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Makkah بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَـنِ الرَّحِيمِ In the Name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. The Believers are tested so that it may be known Who is Sincere and Who is Lying Allah says: الم Alif Lam Mim. In the beginning of the Tafsir of Surah Al-Baqarah, we discussed the letters which appear at the beginning of some Surahs. أَحَسِبَ النَّاسُ أَن يُتْرَكُواْ أَن …

**Muyassar:** أظَنَّ الناس إذ قالوا: آمنا، أن الله يتركهم بلا ابتلاء ولا اختبار؟

### Ayah 3

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Makkah بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَـنِ الرَّحِيمِ In the Name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. The Believers are tested so that it may be known Who is Sincere and Who is Lying Allah says: الم Alif Lam Mim. **Muyassar:** ولقد فتنَّا الذين من قبلهم من الأمم واختبرناهم، ممن أرسلنا إليهم رسلنا، فليعلمنَّ الله علمًا ظاهرًا للخلق صدق الصادقين في إيمانهم، وكذب الكاذبين؛ ليميز كلَّ فريق من الآخر.
