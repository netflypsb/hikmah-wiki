# آل عمران — Aali Imran (3)

> Aali Imran — 200 verses, Medinan

## Overview

Aali Imran (آل عمران) is the 3rd surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Medinan** surah comprising **200 verses**, spanning **juz 3, 4** (pages 50–76 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **20 rukus**.
