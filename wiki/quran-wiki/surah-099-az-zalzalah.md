# الزلزلة — Az-Zalzalah (99)

> Az-Zalzalah — 8 verses, Medinan

## Overview

Az-Zalzalah (الزلزلة) is the 99th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Medinan** surah comprising **8 verses**, spanning **juz 30** (pages 599–599 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **1 rukus**.
