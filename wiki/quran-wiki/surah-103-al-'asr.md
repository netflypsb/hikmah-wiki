# العصر — Al-'Asr (103)

> Al-'Asr — 3 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Al-'Asr (العصر) is the 103th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **3 verses**, spanning **juz 30** (pages 601–601 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **1 rukus**.
