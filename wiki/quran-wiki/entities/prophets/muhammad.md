# Muhammad (SAW) — The Final Messenger

## Overview

Muhammad (SAW) (مُحَمَّد) is the seal of the prophets (Khatam al-Anbiya), the mercy to the worlds (Rahmatan lil-'Alamin), and the recipient of the final revelation. The Qur'an commands believers to send blessings upon him and obey him as the practical implementation of divine will.

## Lessons from His Story

**Finality is Closure** — No prophet will come after Muhammad; the Shari'ah is complete and eternal.
**Mercy Before Judgement** — His sending is mercy, not condemnation; even the most sinful can repent.
**The Night Journey as Proof** — The Isra and Mi'raj establish that divine power transcends space and time; the five prayers were prescribed directly by Allah without intermediary.
**Love as Obligation** — Sending blessings (salat and salam) is a command, not a recommendation — love for the Prophet is part of iman.
