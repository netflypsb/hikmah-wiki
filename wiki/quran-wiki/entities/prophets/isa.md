# Isa (AS) — Jesus

## Overview

Isa (AS) (عِيسَىٰ) is one of the greatest prophets in Islam — the Messiah (Al-Masih), the Word of Allah (Kalimatullah), and the Spirit from Him (Ruhullah). He was born miraculously to Maryam (AS), performed miracles, was raised to heaven, and will return before the Hour.

## Key Narrative Events

- **Birth / Calling:** [[surah-003-aali-imran|Aali Imran (3:45-51)]], [[surah-019-maryam|Maryam (19:16-34)]] — Born without a father; Maryam's chastity and the angel's annunciation
- **Miracle / Staff:** [[surah-003-aali-imran|Aali Imran (3:49)]], [[surah-005-al-ma'idah|Al-Ma'idah (5:110-111)]] — Heals the blind and leper, raises the dead, creates a bird from clay by Allah's permission
- **Divine Encounter:** N/A
- **Trial / Test:** [[surah-005-al-ma'idah|Al-Ma'idah (5:17, 5:72-75)]], [[surah-009-at-tawbah|At-Tawbah (9:30-31)]] — Explicit rejection of divinity: 'Isa is a servant and messenger of Allah'
- **Resolution:** [[surah-004-an-nisa|An-Nisa (4:157-158)]] — 'They did not kill him, nor did they crucify him, but it was made to appear so... Allah raised him to Himself'

## Lessons from His Story

**Miracle as Sign, Not Proof of Divinity** — Isa's miracles are by Allah's permission, not by independent divine power.
**Chastity and Piety** — Maryam (AS) is the supreme example of female devotion; Isa's birth demonstrates Allah's power over natural law.
**The Cross as Misunderstanding** — The Qur'an explicitly rejects crucifixion, establishing that divine victory does not require apparent worldly defeat.
**Return as Justice** — Isa's second coming will break the cross, kill the swine, and establish justice — a sign of the Hour.
