# Nuh (AS) — Noah

## Overview

Nuh (AS) (نُوح) is the prophet of patience par excellence — he called his people for 950 years with almost no success. His story is one of divine justice (the flood) and divine mercy (the ark and the covenant).

## Key Narrative Events

- **Birth / Calling:** [[surah-029-al-ankabut|Al-Ankabut (29:14-15)]] — 'He remained among them a thousand years less fifty' — 950 years of da'wah
- **Miracle / Staff:** N/A
- **Divine Encounter:** N/A
- **Trial / Test:** [[surah-011-hud|Hud (11:36-48)]], [[surah-023-al-mu'minun|Al-Mu'minun (23:23-30)]] — Building the ark by Allah's command; every creature in pairs; the flood covering the mountains
- **Resolution:** [[surah-011-hud|Hud (11:42-47)]] — Nuh's son drowned despite his plea; Allah's decree overrides even parental love

## Lessons from His Story

**Patience Without Result is Still Reward** — 950 years with minimal response; Allah rewards the effort, not the outcome.
**Obedience Without Understanding** — Building an ark on dry land; faith means acting before the reason is visible.
**Divine Justice Overrides Kinship** — Nuh's son drowned; righteousness is individual, not inherited.
**The Ark as Salvation** — The ark is the Shari'ah: only those who enter it are saved.
