# Musa (AS) — Moses

## Overview

Musa (AS) (مُوسَىٰ) is the most frequently mentioned prophet in the Qur'an — referenced by name 136 times across 34 surahs. He is the speaking interlocutor with Allah (Kalimullah), the liberator of Bani Israel, and the recipient of the Torah.

## Key Narrative Events

- **Birth / Calling:** [[surah-020-ta-ha|Ta-Ha (20)]] — Born under Pharaoh's persecution, placed in the Nile, raised in the palace (20:38-40)
- **Miracle / Staff:** [[surah-020-ta-ha|Ta-Ha (20)]] — The staff turns to a serpent; his hand becomes white without harm (20:17-22)
- **Divine Encounter:** [[surah-004-an-nisa|An-Nisa (4:164)]], [[surah-007-al-a'raf|Al-A'raf (7:143-144)]] — Spoke to Allah directly; asked to see Allah, shown the mountain
- **Trial / Test:** [[surah-007-al-a'raf|Al-A'raf (7:113-122)]], [[surah-020-ta-ha|Ta-Ha (20:70-73)]] — The magicians' conversion at the court of Pharaoh
- **Resolution:** [[surah-026-ash-shu'ara|Ash-Shu'ara (26)]] — The Red Sea split, Pharaoh drowned (26:52-68)

## Lessons from His Story

**Divine Speech** — Musa is the only prophet who spoke to Allah directly (Kalimullah), establishing the legitimacy of revelation.
**Patience in Prophecy** — 950 years of calling Pharaoh with no initial success; victory comes only by divine timing.
**Knowledge vs. Wisdom** — The journey with Khidr demonstrates that apparent divine actions may exceed human comprehension; wisdom requires humility.
**The Staff as Symbol** — The transformation of the staff represents that Allah can turn any humble instrument into a sign of power.
