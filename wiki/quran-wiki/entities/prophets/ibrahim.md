# Ibrahim (AS) — Abraham

## Overview

Ibrahim (AS) (إِبْرَاهِيم) is the friend of Allah (Khalilullah), the father of monotheism (Hanif), and the patriarch of prophets. The Qur'an calls the true religion 'the millah of Ibrahim' and identifies him as the builder of the Ka'bah.

## Lessons from His Story

**Rational Monotheism** — Ibrahim used reason to reject idolatry: 'Do you worship what you carve?' (37:95)
**Submission Over Instinct** — The sacrifice demonstrates that divine command supersedes even parental love.
**Universal Legacy** — The Ka'bah is the qibla for all Muslims; Ibrahim's dua for a messenger was answered in Muhammad (SAW).
**Courage in Opposition** — Alone against his nation and his father, he stood firm — the model of 'Hanif' (pure monotheist).
