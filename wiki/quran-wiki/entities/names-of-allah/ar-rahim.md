# Ar-Rahim — The Most Merciful

## Arabic

**الرَّحِيم**

## Definition

The one whose mercy is specific to the believers in the Hereafter

## Meaning

Ar-Rahim means **The Most Merciful**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
