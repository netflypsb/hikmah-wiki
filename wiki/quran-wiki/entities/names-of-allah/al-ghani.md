# Al-Ghani — The Self-Sufficient

## Arabic

**الْغَنِي**

## Definition

The one absolutely free from need, rich beyond all measure

## Meaning

Al-Ghani means **The Self-Sufficient**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
