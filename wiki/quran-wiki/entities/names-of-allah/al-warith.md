# Al-Warith — The Inheritor

## Arabic

**الْوَارِث**

## Definition

The one who inherits all that remains after creation perishes

## Meaning

Al-Warith means **The Inheritor**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
