# Al-Alim — The All-Knowing

## Arabic

**الْعَلِيم**

## Definition

The one whose knowledge encompasses all things, seen and unseen, past and future

## Meaning

Al-Alim means **The All-Knowing**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
