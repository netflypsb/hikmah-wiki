# Al-Jabbar — The Compeller / The Restorer

## Arabic

**الْجَبَّار**

## Definition

The one whose will none can resist; who compels the broken to wholeness

## Meaning

Al-Jabbar means **The Compeller / The Restorer**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
