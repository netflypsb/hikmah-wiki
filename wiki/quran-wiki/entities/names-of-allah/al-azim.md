# Al-Azim — The Magnificent

## Arabic

**الْعَظِيم**

## Definition

The one of absolute greatness, whose majesty fills all creation

## Meaning

Al-Azim means **The Magnificent**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
