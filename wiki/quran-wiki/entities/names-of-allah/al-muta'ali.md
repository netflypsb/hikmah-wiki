# Al-Muta'ali — The Most Exalted

## Arabic

**الْمُتَعَالِي**

## Definition

The one exalted above all creation, far above any imperfection

## Meaning

Al-Muta'ali means **The Most Exalted**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
