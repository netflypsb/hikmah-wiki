# Al-Basir — The All-Seeing

## Arabic

**الْبَصِير**

## Definition

The one who sees all things, visible and invisible, with perfect clarity

## Meaning

Al-Basir means **The All-Seeing**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
