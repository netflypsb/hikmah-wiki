# Al-Hayy — The Ever-Living

## Arabic

**الْحَي**

## Definition

The one who has life in Himself, eternal and independent, source of all life

## Meaning

Al-Hayy means **The Ever-Living**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
