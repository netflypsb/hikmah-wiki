# Al-Mani' — The Preventer / The Withholder

## Arabic

**الْمَانِع**

## Definition

The one who prevents harm and withholds from whom He wills

## Meaning

Al-Mani' means **The Preventer / The Withholder**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
