# Al-Muqit — The Sustainer

## Arabic

**الْمُقِيت**

## Definition

The one who gives every creature its nourishment and sustenance

## Meaning

Al-Muqit means **The Sustainer**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
