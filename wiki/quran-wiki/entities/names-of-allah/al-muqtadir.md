# Al-Muqtadir — The Omnipotent

## Arabic

**الْمُقْتَدِر**

## Definition

The one whose power is absolute and overwhelming over all creation

## Meaning

Al-Muqtadir means **The Omnipotent**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
