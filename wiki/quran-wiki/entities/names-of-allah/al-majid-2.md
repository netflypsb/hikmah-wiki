# Al-Majid (2) — The Illustrious / The Noble

## Arabic

**الْمَاجِد**

## Definition

The one of absolute nobility and high standing

## Meaning

Al-Majid (2) means **The Illustrious / The Noble**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
