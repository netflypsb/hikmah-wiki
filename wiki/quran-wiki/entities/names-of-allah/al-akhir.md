# Al-Akhir — The Last

## Arabic

**الْآخِر**

## Definition

The one after whom nothing will remain; eternal without end

## Meaning

Al-Akhir means **The Last**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
