# Al-Mutakabbir — The Majestic / The Supreme

## Arabic

**الْمُتَكَبِّر**

## Definition

The one supremely great, above all false claims of greatness

## Meaning

Al-Mutakabbir means **The Majestic / The Supreme**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
