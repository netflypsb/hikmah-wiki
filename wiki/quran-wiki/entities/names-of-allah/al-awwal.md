# Al-Awwal — The First

## Arabic

**الْأَوَّل**

## Definition

The one before whom nothing existed; eternal without beginning

## Meaning

Al-Awwal means **The First**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
