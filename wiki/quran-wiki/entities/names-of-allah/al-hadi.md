# Al-Hadi — The Guide

## Arabic

**الْهَادِي**

## Definition

The one who guides whom He wills to the straight path of truth

## Meaning

Al-Hadi means **The Guide**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
