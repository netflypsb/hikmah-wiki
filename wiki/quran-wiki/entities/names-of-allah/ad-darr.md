# Ad-Darr — The Distressor

## Arabic

**الضَّار**

## Definition

The one who creates hardship as a test and means of return to Him

## Meaning

Ad-Darr means **The Distressor**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
