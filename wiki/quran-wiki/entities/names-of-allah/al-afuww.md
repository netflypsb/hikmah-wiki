# Al-Afuww — The Pardoner

## Arabic

**الْعَفُو**

## Definition

The one who erases sins completely, leaving no trace

## Meaning

Al-Afuww means **The Pardoner**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
