# At-Tawwab — The Acceptor of Repentance

## Arabic

**التَّوَّاب**

## Definition

The one who constantly accepts repentance and returns sinners to Him

## Meaning

At-Tawwab means **The Acceptor of Repentance**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
