# Al-Adl — The Just

## Arabic

**الْعَدْل**

## Definition

The one in whom absolute justice resides; never wrongs any soul

## Meaning

Al-Adl means **The Just**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
