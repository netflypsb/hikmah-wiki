# Al-Aziz — The Almighty / The Invincible

## Arabic

**الْعَزِيز**

## Definition

The one of absolute might and honor, whom none can overpower

## Meaning

Al-Aziz means **The Almighty / The Invincible**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
