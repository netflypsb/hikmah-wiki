# Al-Mumit — The Bringer of Death

## Arabic

**الْمُمِيت**

## Definition

The one who causes death, the appointed end for all living things

## Meaning

Al-Mumit means **The Bringer of Death**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
