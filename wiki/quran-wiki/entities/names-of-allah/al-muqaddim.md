# Al-Muqaddim — The Expediter

## Arabic

**الْمُقَدِّم**

## Definition

The one who advances whom He wills in rank and goodness

## Meaning

Al-Muqaddim means **The Expediter**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
