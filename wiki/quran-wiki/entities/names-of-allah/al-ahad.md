# Al-Ahad — The Unique / The Absolute One

## Arabic

**الْأَحَد**

## Definition

The one who is alone in divinity, indivisible and incomparable

## Meaning

Al-Ahad means **The Unique / The Absolute One**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
