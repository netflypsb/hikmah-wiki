# Al-Hakim — The All-Wise

## Arabic

**الْحَكِيم**

## Definition

The one in whom perfect wisdom resides; every action is purposeful

## Meaning

Al-Hakim means **The All-Wise**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
