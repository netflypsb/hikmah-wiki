# Al-Mu'min — The Granter of Security

## Arabic

**الْمُؤْمِن**

## Definition

The one who grants security and freedom from fear

## Meaning

Al-Mu'min means **The Granter of Security**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
