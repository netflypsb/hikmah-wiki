# Names of Allah — Asma ul-Husna

## Overview

The Quran mentions approximately 99 names and attributes of Allah (Asma ul-Husna). These names describe His essence, actions, and relationships with creation. They are the foundation of tawhid and the subject of deep reflection (tafakkur).
