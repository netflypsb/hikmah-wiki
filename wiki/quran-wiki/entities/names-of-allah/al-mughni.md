# Al-Mughni — The Enricher

## Arabic

**الْمُغْنِي**

## Definition

The one who enriches whom He wills, granting wealth of soul and provision

## Meaning

Al-Mughni means **The Enricher**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
