# Malik al-Mulk — The Owner of All Sovereignty

## Arabic

**مَالِكُ الْمُلْك**

## Definition

The sole possessor of all dominion, giving it to whom He wills

## Meaning

Malik al-Mulk means **The Owner of All Sovereignty**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
