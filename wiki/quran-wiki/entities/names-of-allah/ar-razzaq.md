# Ar-Razzaq — The Provider

## Arabic

**الرَّزَّاق**

## Definition

The one who provides sustenance to all creation without limit

## Meaning

Ar-Razzaq means **The Provider**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
