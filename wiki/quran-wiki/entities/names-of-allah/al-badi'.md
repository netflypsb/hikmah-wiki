# Al-Badi' — The Incomparable

## Arabic

**الْبَدِيع**

## Definition

The one who creates without precedent, whose works are unique and wondrous

## Meaning

Al-Badi' means **The Incomparable**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
