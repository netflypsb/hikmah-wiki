# Al-Ghaffar — The Ever-Forgiving

## Arabic

**الْغَفَّار**

## Definition

The one who repeatedly forgives sins, covering them with His mercy

## Meaning

Al-Ghaffar means **The Ever-Forgiving**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
