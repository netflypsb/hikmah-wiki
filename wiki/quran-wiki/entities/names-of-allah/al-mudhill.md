# Al-Mudhill — The Humiliator

## Arabic

**الْمُذِلُّ**

## Definition

The one who dishonors whom He wills, separating them from His mercy

## Meaning

Al-Mudhill means **The Humiliator**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
