# Ar-Rashid — The Guide to the Right Path

## Arabic

**الرَّشِيد**

## Definition

The one who guides to what is right and proper in all affairs

## Meaning

Ar-Rashid means **The Guide to the Right Path**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
