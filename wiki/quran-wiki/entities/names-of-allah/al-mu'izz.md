# Al-Mu'izz — The Honorer

## Arabic

**الْمُعِزُّ**

## Definition

The one who gives honor and dignity to whom He wills

## Meaning

Al-Mu'izz means **The Honorer**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
