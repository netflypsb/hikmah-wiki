# Al-Wajid — The Finder / The All-Perceiving

## Arabic

**الْوَاجِد**

## Definition

The one who finds and attains all things, never lacking

## Meaning

Al-Wajid means **The Finder / The All-Perceiving**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
