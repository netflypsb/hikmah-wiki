# An-Nur — The Light

## Arabic

**النُّور**

## Definition

The source of all light in heavens and earth, physical and spiritual

## Meaning

An-Nur means **The Light**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
