# As-Sabur — The Patient

## Arabic

**الصَّبُور**

## Definition

The one who delays punishment, granting time for repentance with infinite patience

## Meaning

As-Sabur means **The Patient**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
