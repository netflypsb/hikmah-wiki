# Al-Mubdi' — The Originator

## Arabic

**الْمُبْدِئ**

## Definition

The one who originates creation from nothing, beginning all things

## Meaning

Al-Mubdi' means **The Originator**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
