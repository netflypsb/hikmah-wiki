# Dhu al-Jalal wal-Ikram — The Lord of Majesty and Honor

## Arabic

**ذُو الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَام**

## Definition

The one possessing absolute majesty and bestowing honor

## Meaning

Dhu al-Jalal wal-Ikram means **The Lord of Majesty and Honor**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
