# Ar-Rafi' — The Exalter

## Arabic

**الرَّافِع**

## Definition

The one who raises the ranks of the righteous and elevates the humble

## Meaning

Ar-Rafi' means **The Exalter**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
