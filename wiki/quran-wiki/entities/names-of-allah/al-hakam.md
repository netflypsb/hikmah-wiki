# Al-Hakam — The Judge

## Arabic

**الْحَكَم**

## Definition

The absolute judge whose verdicts are perfect justice

## Meaning

Al-Hakam means **The Judge**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
