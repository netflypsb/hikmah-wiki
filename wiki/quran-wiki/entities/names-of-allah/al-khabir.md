# Al-Khabir — The All-Aware

## Arabic

**الْخَبِير**

## Definition

The one perfectly aware of the inner reality and secrets of all things

## Meaning

Al-Khabir means **The All-Aware**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
