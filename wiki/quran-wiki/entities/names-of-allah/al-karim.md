# Al-Karim — The Generous

## Arabic

**الْكَرِيم**

## Definition

The one who gives abundantly without being asked, the most noble in character

## Meaning

Al-Karim means **The Generous**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
