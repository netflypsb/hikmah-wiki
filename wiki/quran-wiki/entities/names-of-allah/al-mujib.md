# Al-Mujib — The Responsive

## Arabic

**الْمُجِيب**

## Definition

The one who responds to the calls of those who call upon Him

## Meaning

Al-Mujib means **The Responsive**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
