# Al-Kabir — The Greatest

## Arabic

**الْكَبِير**

## Definition

The one whose greatness is absolute, surpassing all standards of magnitude

## Meaning

Al-Kabir means **The Greatest**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
