# Al-Ali — The Most High

## Arabic

**الْعَلِي**

## Definition

The one whose essence and attributes are exalted above all creation

## Meaning

Al-Ali means **The Most High**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
