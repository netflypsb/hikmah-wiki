# Al-Qayyum — The Self-Subsisting

## Arabic

**الْقَيُّوم**

## Definition

The one who stands in Himself, sustaining all else; the eternal maintainer

## Meaning

Al-Qayyum means **The Self-Subsisting**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
