# Ar-Ra'uf — The Compassionate

## Arabic

**الرَّءُوف**

## Definition

The one of intense mercy and tenderness toward His servants

## Meaning

Ar-Ra'uf means **The Compassionate**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
