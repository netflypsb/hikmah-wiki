# Al-Fattah — The Opener / The Judge

## Arabic

**الْفَتَّاح**

## Definition

The one who opens doors of mercy and provision; who decides all judgments

## Meaning

Al-Fattah means **The Opener / The Judge**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
