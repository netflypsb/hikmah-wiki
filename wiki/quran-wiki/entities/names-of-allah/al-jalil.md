# Al-Jalil — The Majestic / The Glorious

## Arabic

**الْجَلِيل**

## Definition

The one of absolute majesty and glory, revered above all

## Meaning

Al-Jalil means **The Majestic / The Glorious**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
