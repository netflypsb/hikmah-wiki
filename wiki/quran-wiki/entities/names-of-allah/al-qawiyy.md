# Al-Qawiyy — The All-Strong

## Arabic

**الْقَوِي**

## Definition

The one of absolute strength and power, whom none can resist

## Meaning

Al-Qawiyy means **The All-Strong**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
