# Al-Qadir — The All-Powerful

## Arabic

**الْقَادِر**

## Definition

The one who has power over all things, able to do whatever He wills

## Meaning

Al-Qadir means **The All-Powerful**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
