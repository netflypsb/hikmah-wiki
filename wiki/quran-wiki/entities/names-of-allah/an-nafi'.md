# An-Nafi' — The Benefactor

## Arabic

**النَّافِع**

## Definition

The one who creates benefit and goodness for whom He wills

## Meaning

An-Nafi' means **The Benefactor**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
