# Al-Mu'akhkhir — The Delayer

## Arabic

**الْمُؤَخِّر**

## Definition

The one who postpones whom He wills, lowering them or delaying their end

## Meaning

Al-Mu'akhkhir means **The Delayer**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
