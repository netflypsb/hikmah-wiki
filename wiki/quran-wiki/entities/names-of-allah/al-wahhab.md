# Al-Wahhab — The Supreme Bestower

## Arabic

**الْوَهَّاب**

## Definition

The one who gives freely without expectation, the most generous giver

## Meaning

Al-Wahhab means **The Supreme Bestower**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
