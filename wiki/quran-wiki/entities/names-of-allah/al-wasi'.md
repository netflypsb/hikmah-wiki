# Al-Wasi' — The All-Encompassing

## Arabic

**الْوَاسِع**

## Definition

The one whose mercy, knowledge, and provision encompass all things

## Meaning

Al-Wasi' means **The All-Encompassing**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
