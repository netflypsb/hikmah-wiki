# Al-Wadud — The Loving

## Arabic

**الْوَدُود**

## Definition

The one who loves the believers and is beloved by them

## Meaning

Al-Wadud means **The Loving**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
