# Al-Malik — The King / Sovereign

## Arabic

**الْمَلِك**

## Definition

The absolute owner and sovereign of all that exists

## Meaning

Al-Malik means **The King / Sovereign**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
