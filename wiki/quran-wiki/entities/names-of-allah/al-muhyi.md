# Al-Muhyi — The Giver of Life

## Arabic

**الْمُحْيِي**

## Definition

The one who gives life to the dead and animates all living things

## Meaning

Al-Muhyi means **The Giver of Life**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
