# As-Sami' — The All-Hearing

## Arabic

**السَّمِيع**

## Definition

The one who hears all sounds, voices, and the whisper of hearts

## Meaning

As-Sami' means **The All-Hearing**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
