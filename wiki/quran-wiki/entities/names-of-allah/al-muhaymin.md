# Al-Muhaymin — The Guardian / The Witness

## Arabic

**الْمُهَيْمِن**

## Definition

The ever-watchful guardian over all things

## Meaning

Al-Muhaymin means **The Guardian / The Witness**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
