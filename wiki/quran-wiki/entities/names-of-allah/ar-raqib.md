# Ar-Raqib — The Watchful

## Arabic

**الرَّقِيب**

## Definition

The one who watches over all creatures, never neglecting any affair

## Meaning

Ar-Raqib means **The Watchful**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
