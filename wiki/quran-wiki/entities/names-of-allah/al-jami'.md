# Al-Jami' — The Gatherer

## Arabic

**الْجَامِع**

## Definition

The one who gathers all creation on the Day of Judgment and all scattered things

## Meaning

Al-Jami' means **The Gatherer**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
