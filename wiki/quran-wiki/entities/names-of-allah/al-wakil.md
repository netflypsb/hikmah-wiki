# Al-Wakil — The Trustee

## Arabic

**الْوَكِيل**

## Definition

The one who is sufficient as trustee and guardian for all who rely on Him

## Meaning

Al-Wakil means **The Trustee**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
