# Al-Wali — The Governor / The Patron

## Arabic

**الْوَالِي**

## Definition

The one who manages all affairs and governs all things

## Meaning

Al-Wali means **The Governor / The Patron**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
