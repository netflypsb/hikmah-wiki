# Al-Khaliq — The Creator

## Arabic

**الْخَالِق**

## Definition

The one who brings into existence from non-existence, measuring and proportioning

## Meaning

Al-Khaliq means **The Creator**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
