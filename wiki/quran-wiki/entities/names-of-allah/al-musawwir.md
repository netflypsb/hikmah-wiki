# Al-Musawwir — The Fashioner / The Shaper

## Arabic

**الْمُصَوِّر**

## Definition

The one who gives form and shape to all creation

## Meaning

Al-Musawwir means **The Fashioner / The Shaper**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
