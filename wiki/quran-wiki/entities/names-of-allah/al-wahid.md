# Al-Wahid — The One

## Arabic

**الْوَاحِد**

## Definition

The one without partner, unique in essence and attributes

## Meaning

Al-Wahid means **The One**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
