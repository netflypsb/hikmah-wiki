# Al-Baqiy — The Everlasting

## Arabic

**الْبَاقِي**

## Definition

The one whose existence and attributes remain forever without change

## Meaning

Al-Baqiy means **The Everlasting**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
