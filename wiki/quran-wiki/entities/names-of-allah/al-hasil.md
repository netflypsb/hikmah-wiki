# Al-Hasil — The Reckoner

## Arabic

**الْحَسِيب**

## Definition

The one who takes account of all deeds with perfect precision

## Meaning

Al-Hasil means **The Reckoner**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
