# Al-Batin — The Hidden

## Arabic

**الْبَاطِن**

## Definition

The one hidden from direct perception, known through His signs and attributes

## Meaning

Al-Batin means **The Hidden**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
