# Al-Mu'id — The Restorer

## Arabic

**الْمُعِيد**

## Definition

The one who restores creation after death, bringing it back to life

## Meaning

Al-Mu'id means **The Restorer**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
