# Al-Muntaqim — The Avenger

## Arabic

**الْمُنْتَقِم**

## Definition

The one who punishes wrongdoers with justice, protecting the oppressed

## Meaning

Al-Muntaqim means **The Avenger**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
