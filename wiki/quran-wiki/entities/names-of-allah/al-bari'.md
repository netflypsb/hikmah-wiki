# Al-Bari' — The Evolver / The Maker

## Arabic

**الْبَارِئ**

## Definition

The one who creates with perfect order and free from any pre-existing model

## Meaning

Al-Bari' means **The Evolver / The Maker**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
