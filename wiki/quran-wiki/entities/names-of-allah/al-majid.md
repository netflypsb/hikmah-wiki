# Al-Majid — The Glorious

## Arabic

**الْمَجِيد**

## Definition

The one of absolute glory and nobility, whose deeds are magnificent

## Meaning

Al-Majid means **The Glorious**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
