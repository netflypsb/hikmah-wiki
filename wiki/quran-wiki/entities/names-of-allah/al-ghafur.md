# Al-Ghafur — The Forgiving

## Arabic

**الْغَفُور**

## Definition

The one who forgives sins and covers them completely

## Meaning

Al-Ghafur means **The Forgiving**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
