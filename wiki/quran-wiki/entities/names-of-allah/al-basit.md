# Al-Basit — The Expander

## Arabic

**الْبَاسِط**

## Definition

The one who expands provision, mercy, and blessings without limit

## Meaning

Al-Basit means **The Expander**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
