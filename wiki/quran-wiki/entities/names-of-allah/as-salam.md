# As-Salam — The Source of Peace

## Arabic

**السَّلَام**

## Definition

The one who is free from all defects, and from whom all peace emanates

## Meaning

As-Salam means **The Source of Peace**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
