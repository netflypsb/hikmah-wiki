# Al-Barr — The Source of Goodness

## Arabic

**الْبَر**

## Definition

The one who is kind and benevolent to His servants, bestowing goodness

## Meaning

Al-Barr means **The Source of Goodness**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
