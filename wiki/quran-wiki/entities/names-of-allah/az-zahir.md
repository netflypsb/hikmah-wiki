# Az-Zahir — The Manifest

## Arabic

**الظَّاهِر**

## Definition

The one whose existence and signs are evident throughout creation

## Meaning

Az-Zahir means **The Manifest**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
