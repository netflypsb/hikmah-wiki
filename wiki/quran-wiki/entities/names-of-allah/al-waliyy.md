# Al-Waliyy — The Protecting Friend

## Arabic

**الْوَلِي**

## Definition

The guardian and ally of the righteous, guiding and protecting them

## Meaning

Al-Waliyy means **The Protecting Friend**. It is one of the names by which Allah has described Himself in the Quran and through the revelation to the Prophet Muhammad (SAW).
