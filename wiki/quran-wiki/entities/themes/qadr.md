# Qadr — Divine Decree

## Definition

Qadr (قَدَر) is Allah's complete knowledge, power, and will over all things — the measure, timing, and destination of every event. It is one of the six pillars of iman and a frequent Qur'anic theme.

## Etymology

ق-د-ر (qadara) — to measure, determine, estimate, decree with power.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2)]] — 'Allah has power over all things' (2:20, 2:106, 2:148, 2:259)
- [[surah-054-al-qamar|Al-Qamar (54)]] — 'And We have made the Qur'an easy for remembrance — is there any who will remember?' (54:17, 22, 32, 40) — repeated decree
- [[surah-097-al-qadr|Al-Qadr (97)]] — The night of decree, better than a thousand months
- [[surah-065-at-talaq|At-Talaq (65)]] — 'Allah has decreed for you the dissolution of your oaths' (65:1)
