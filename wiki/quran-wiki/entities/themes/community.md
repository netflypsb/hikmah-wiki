# Community — Ummah

## Definition

The Muslim community (أُمَّة) is the brotherhood of believers united by faith in Allah and His Messenger. The Qur'an establishes communal obligations: mutual support, consultation (shura), and collective responsibility.

## Etymology

أ-م-م (umma) — to guide, lead, follow; ummah as the community led by divine guidance.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2:143)]] — 'And thus We have made you a middle nation that you will be witnesses over the people'
- [[surah-003-aali-imran|Aali Imran (3:103)]] — 'And hold fast to the rope of Allah all together and do not become divided'
- [[surah-049-al-hujurat|Al-Hujurat (49:10)]] — 'The believers are but brothers, so make peace between your brothers'
