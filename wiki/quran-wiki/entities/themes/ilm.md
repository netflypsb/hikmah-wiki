# Ilm — Knowledge

## Definition

Ilm (عِلْم) is knowledge of Allah, His signs, His Book, and His creation. The Qur'an repeatedly commands seeking knowledge and praises the ulama (those with knowledge) above the ignorant.

## Etymology

ع-ل-م (alima) — to know, to learn, to teach, to mark.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2)]] — 'And say: My Lord, increase me in knowledge' (20:114) — actually Surah Ta-Ha
- [[surah-058-al-mujadila|Al-Mujadila (58)]] — 'Allah raises those who have believed among you and those who were given knowledge by degrees' (58:11)
- [[surah-096-al-alaq|Al-'Alaq (96)]] — 'Read in the name of your Lord who created... He taught by the pen, taught man what he knew not' (96:1, 96:4-5) — the first revelation is about knowledge
- [[surah-035-fatir|Fatir (35)]] — 'Allah is the All-Knower of the unseen of the heavens and earth' (35:38)
