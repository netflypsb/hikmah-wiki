# Protection — Isti'adha

## Definition

Divine protection (اسْتِعَاذَة) is seeking refuge in Allah from evil, harm, and the whisperings of Shaytan. The Mu'awwidhatan (Al-Falaq and An-Nas) are the primary surahs of protection.

## Etymology

ع-و-ذ (a'adha) — to seek refuge, protect, shelter; isti'adha as the act of seeking divine refuge.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-113-al-falaq|Al-Falaq (113)]] — 'Say: I seek refuge in the Lord of daybreak'
- [[surah-114-an-nas|An-Nas (114)]] — 'Say: I seek refuge in the Lord of mankind'
- [[surah-007-al-a'raf|Al-A'raf (7:200)]] — 'And if an evil suggestion comes to you from Shaytan, seek refuge in Allah'
