# Prohibition — Nahy

## Definition

Prohibition (نَهْي) is the divine forbidding of shirk, immorality, injustice, and all acts that harm the soul or society. It complements command (amr) as the negative boundary of divine law.

## Etymology

ن-ه-ي (naha) — to forbid, prevent, restrain, protect.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-004-an-nisa|An-Nisa (4:19)]] — 'And do not inherit women by compulsion'
- [[surah-006-al-an'am|Al-An'am (6:151)]] — 'Say: Come, I will recite what your Lord has prohibited to you'
- [[surah-007-al-a'raf|Al-A'raf (7:33)]] — 'Say: My Lord has only forbidden immoralities'
