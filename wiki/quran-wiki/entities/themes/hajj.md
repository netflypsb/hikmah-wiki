# Hajj — Pilgrimage

## Definition

Hajj (حَجّ) is the annual pilgrimage to the sacred House (Ka'bah) in Makkah, one of the five pillars of Islam. It commemorates the trials of Ibrahim (AS) and his family and unites Muslims from every nation.

## Etymology

ح-ج-ج (hajja) — to intend, visit, circumambulate; hajj as the intended journey to the sacred House.
