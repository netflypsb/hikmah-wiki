# Warning — Wa'id

## Definition

Divine warning (وَعِيد) is the threat of punishment for disbelief, disobedience, and injustice. It is paired with promise (wa'd) to present the two outcomes of human choice.

## Etymology

و-ع-د (wa'ada) — to promise, threaten, make a covenant.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2:39)]] — 'But those who disbelieve and deny Our signs — they are the companions of the Fire'
- [[surah-067-al-mulk|Al-Mulk (67)]] — Full surah of warnings: 'He who created death and life to test you'
- [[surah-104-al-humazah|Al-Humazah (104)]] — 'Woe to every slanderer and mocker'
