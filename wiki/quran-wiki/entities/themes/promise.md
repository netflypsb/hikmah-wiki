# Promise — Wa'd

## Definition

Divine promise (وَعْد) is the guarantee of reward, paradise, and mercy for the believers. It is the positive counterpart to warning (wa'id), representing the fruit of faith and righteous action.

## Etymology

و-ع-د (wa'ada) — to promise, pledge, assure.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2:25)]] — 'And give glad tidings to those who believe and do righteous deeds'
- [[surah-039-az-zumar|Az-Zumar (39:20)]] — 'But those who fear their Lord will have gardens built upon gardens'
- [[surah-055-ar-rahman|Ar-Rahman (55:46-77)]] — Detailed description of the two gardens for the righteous
