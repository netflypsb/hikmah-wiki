# Story — Qasas

## Definition

The Quranic story (قَصَص) is not biography for its own sake but spiritual instruction through narrative. Stories of prophets, nations, and individuals demonstrate divine justice, mercy, and the consequences of belief and disbelief.

## Etymology

ق-ص-ص (qassa) — to recount, narrate, follow in sequence.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-012-yusuf|Yusuf (12:3)]] — 'We relate to you the best of stories'
- [[surah-011-hud|Hud (11:120)]] — 'And each story We relate to you from the messengers'
- [[surah-028-al-qasas|Al-Qasas (28)]] — The surah named 'The Stories'
