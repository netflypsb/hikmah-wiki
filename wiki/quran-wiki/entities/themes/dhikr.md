# Dhikr — Remembrance of Allah

## Definition

Dhikr (ذِكْر) is the remembrance, mention, and mindfulness of Allah. It is the spiritual practice that elevates the heart, protects from heedlessness, and draws divine mercy.

## Etymology

ذ-ك-ر (dhakara) — to remember, to mention, to remind; the root of dhikr and tadhkir.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-013-ar-ra'd|Ar-Ra'd (13:28)]] — 'Those who have believed and whose hearts are assured by the remembrance of Allah'
- [[surah-033-al-ahzab|Al-Ahzab (33:41)]] — 'O you who have believed, remember Allah with much remembrance'
- [[surah-063-al-munafiqun|Al-Munafiqun (63:9)]] — 'O you who have believed, let not your wealth and your children divert you from the remembrance of Allah'
