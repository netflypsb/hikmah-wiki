# Law — Hukm / Shari'ah

## Definition

Divine law (حُكْم) in the Qur'an encompasses all legislated matters: worship (ibadat), transactions (muamalat), family law (munakahat), and criminal law (jinayat). It is primarily revealed in Madani surahs.

## Etymology

ح-ك-م (hakama) — to judge, rule, decide, govern.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-004-an-nisa|An-Nisa (4)]] — Comprehensive family and criminal law
- [[surah-005-al-ma'idah|Al-Ma'idah (5)]] — Final legal rulings, food laws, retribution
- [[surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2:178-179)]] — Prescribed legal retribution (qisas)
