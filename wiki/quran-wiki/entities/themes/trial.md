# Trial — Ibtida / Fitna

## Definition

Divine trial (ابْتِلَاء / فِتْنَة) is the testing of faith through hardship, prosperity, temptation, or persecution. Trials are not punishments but opportunities for spiritual growth and proof of sincerity.

## Etymology

ب-ت-ل (batala) — to test, examine, prove; ف-ت-ن (fatana) — to test, tempt, refine by fire.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2:155)]] — 'And We will surely test you with something of fear and hunger'
- [[surah-018-al-kahf|Al-Kahf (18:7)]] — 'Indeed We have made that which is on earth as adornment for it, that We may test them'
- [[surah-029-al-ankabut|Al-Ankabut (29:2-3)]] — 'Do the people think that they will be left to say We believe and they will not be tried?'
