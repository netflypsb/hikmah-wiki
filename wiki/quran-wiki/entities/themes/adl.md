# Adl — Justice

## Definition

Adl (عَدْل) is justice, equity, and proportion — placing things in their rightful place. It is one of the names of Allah (Al-Adl) and a central command in the Qur'an.

## Etymology

ع-د-ل (adala) — to be just, equitable, balanced; the opposite of zulm (wrongdoing).

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-004-an-nisa|An-Nisa (4:135)]] — 'O you who have believed, be persistently standing firm in justice'
- [[surah-005-al-ma'idah|Al-Ma'idah (5:8)]] — 'Be just; that is nearer to righteousness'
- [[surah-016-an-nahl|An-Nahl (16:90)]] — 'Indeed Allah commands justice and good conduct'
