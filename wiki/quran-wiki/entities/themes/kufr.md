# Kufr — Disbelief

## Definition

Kufr (كُفْر) is the rejection, denial, or covering of divine truth. It is not merely intellectual doubt but the active refusal to submit to Allah after the truth has been made clear.

## Etymology

ك-ف-ر (kafara) — to cover, conceal, reject; kafir as one who covers the truth with disbelief.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2:6-7)]] — 'Indeed those who disbelieve — it is all the same to them whether you warn them or do not warn them'
- [[surah-003-aali-imran|Aali Imran (3:90)]] — 'Indeed those who disbelieve after their belief then increase in disbelief'
- [[surah-047-muhammad|Muhammad (47:32)]] — 'Those who disbelieve and avert from the way of Allah'
