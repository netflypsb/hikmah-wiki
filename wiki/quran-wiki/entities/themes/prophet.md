# Prophet — Nabi

## Definition

The prophet (نَبِيّ) is the human messenger chosen by Allah to convey divine guidance. The Qur'an mentions 25 prophets by name and affirms that every nation received a messenger.

## Etymology

ن-ب-أ (naba'a) — to announce, inform, convey news.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2:213)]] — 'Mankind was one community; then Allah sent prophets'
- [[surah-021-al-anbiya|Al-Anbiya (21:25)]] — 'We sent no messenger before you except that We revealed to him'
- [[surah-033-al-ahzab|Al-Ahzab (33:40)]] — 'Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah and the seal of the prophets'
