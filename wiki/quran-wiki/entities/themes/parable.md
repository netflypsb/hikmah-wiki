# Parable — Mathal

## Definition

The Quranic parable (مَثَل) is a metaphorical narrative that illustrates spiritual truths through familiar images: gardens, seeds, light, darkness, rain, trade, agriculture. It is a primary Makki teaching device.

## Etymology

م-ث-ل (mathala) — to resemble, compare, liken; mathal as the comparable illustration.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2:17-18)]] — 'Their example is that of one who kindled a fire'
- [[surah-013-ar-ra'd|Ar-Ra'd (13:17)]] — 'He sends down from the sky rain, and valleys flow according to their capacity'
- [[surah-024-an-nur|An-Nur (24:35)]] — 'Allah is the Light of the heavens and the earth — the parable of His light'
