# Akhirah — The Afterlife

## Definition

Akhirah (الآخِرَة) refers to the life after death: resurrection, reckoning, the Scale of deeds, the Sirat, and eternal abode in Jannah or Jahannam. It is the second major theme of the Qur'an, appearing in virtually every surah.

## Etymology

آ-خ-ر (akhir) — the last, the end, the final destination.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2)]] — 'And guard yourselves against a Day when no soul will avail another' (2:48)
- [[surah-036-ya-sin|Ya-Sin (36)]] — 'The Hour is coming — I almost conceal it' (36:12)
- [[surah-056-al-waqi'a|Al-Waqi'a (56)]] — Full depiction of the three classes on the Day of Sorting
- [[surah-067-al-mulk|Al-Mulk (67)]] — 'He who created death and life to test you' (67:2)
- [[surah-078-an-naba|An-Naba (78)]] — The Day of Decision, the weighing of deeds, the distribution to gardens or blazing fire

## Tafsir Perspectives

Tafsir Ibn Kathir on 2:48: 'Allah warns His servants to prepare for the Day when no intercession, ransom, or kinship will avail...' The akhirah is the ultimate justice that rectifies all worldly inequity.
