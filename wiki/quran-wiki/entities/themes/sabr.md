# Sabr — Patience and Steadfastness

## Definition

Sabr (صَبْر) is enduring hardship with faith, restraining from sin, and persisting in obedience. It is one of the greatest virtues in Islam, paired with gratitude (shukr) as the two wings of the believer.

## Etymology

ص-ب-ر (sabara) — to bind, restrain, endure; the tying of the camel ( tying the self to Allah's command).

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2)]] — 'And seek help in patience and prayer' (2:45)
- [[surah-003-aali-imran|Aali Imran (3)]] — 'Indeed the patient will be given their reward without account' (3:185, variant: 39:10)
- [[surah-012-yusuf|Yusuf (12)]] — The supreme example of sabr: 'And Allah is the best of planners' (12:76, 12:83, 12:101)
- [[surah-094-ash-sharh|Ash-Sharh (94)]] — 'Indeed with hardship comes ease' (94:5-6)

## Tafsir Perspectives

Tafsir Ibn Kathir on 2:45: 'Patience in the face of trials, patience in avoiding sins, and patience in performing acts of obedience... These are the three types of sabr mentioned by the scholars.'
