# Salah — Prayer

## Definition

Salah (صَلَاة) is the prescribed worship of Allah through standing, bowing, prostrating, and supplicating at fixed times. It is the pillar of the religion, mentioned directly in the Qur'an over 80 times and established in every surah that legislates law.

## Etymology

ص-ل-و (saliya) — to invoke, bless, connect; the link between servant and Lord.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2)]] — 'Establish prayer and give zakat' (2:43, 2:110, 2:277)
- [[surah-004-an-nisa|An-Nisa (4)]] — 'When you stand for prayer, wash your faces and hands...' (4:43) — ablution
- [[surah-011-hud|Hud (11)]] — 'And establish prayer at the two ends of the day and at the approach of the night' (11:114)
- [[surah-020-ta-ha|Ta-Ha (20)]] — 'Indeed I am Allah. There is no deity except Me, so worship Me and establish prayer for My remembrance' (20:14)
- [[surah-029-al-ankabut|Al-Ankabut (29)]] — 'And establish prayer. Indeed prayer prohibits immorality and wrongdoing' (29:45)
