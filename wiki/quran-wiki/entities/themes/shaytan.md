# Shaytan — The Whisperer

## Definition

Shaytan (شَيْطَان) is the rebellious jinn (Iblis) and his progeny who whisper evil suggestions, incite to sin, and seek to mislead humanity from the straight path. The Qur'an repeatedly commands seeking refuge from him.

## Etymology

ش-ط-ن (shatana) — to be distant, far removed; shaytan as one far from divine mercy.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2:168)]] — 'O mankind, eat from whatever is on earth that is lawful and good, and do not follow the footsteps of Shaytan'
- [[surah-114-an-nas|An-Nas (114:4-6)]] — 'From the evil of the retreating whisperer — who whispers into the breasts of mankind — among the jinn and mankind'
- [[surah-007-al-a'raf|Al-A'raf (7:200)]] — 'And if an evil suggestion comes to you from Shaytan, seek refuge in Allah'
