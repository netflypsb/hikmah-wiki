# Jumu'ah — Friday Prayer

## Definition

Jumu'ah (جُمُعَة) is the Friday congregational prayer, replacing Dhuhr on that day. It includes a sermon (khutbah), communal prayer, and is a weekly gathering for Islamic instruction and brotherhood.

## Etymology

ج-م-ع (jama'a) — to gather, assemble, collect; yawm al-jumu'ah as the day of gathering.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-062-al-jumu'ah|Al-Jumu'ah (62:9-11)]] — 'O you who have believed, when the call to prayer is made for Jumu'ah, proceed to the remembrance of Allah'
- [[surah-062-al-jumu'ah|Al-Jumu'ah (62:1-8)]] — 'Whatever is in the heavens and earth exalts Allah' — the surah of Jumu'ah
