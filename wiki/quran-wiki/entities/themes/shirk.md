# Shirk — Polytheism

## Definition

Shirk (شِرْك) is the association of partners with Allah in His Lordship, worship, or attributes. It is the opposite of tawhid and the unforgivable sin if one dies without repenting.

## Etymology

ش-ر-ك (sharika) — to share, partner, associate; shirk as the illegitimate sharing of divine sovereignty.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-004-an-nisa|An-Nisa (4:48, 4:116)]] — 'Indeed Allah does not forgive association with Him, but He forgives what is less than that'
- [[surah-006-al-an'am|Al-An'am (6:151)]] — 'Say: Come, I will recite what your Lord has prohibited... that you not associate anything with Him'
- [[surah-112-al-ikhlas|Al-Ikhlas (112)]] — 'He begets not, nor is He begotten, and there is none like Him'
