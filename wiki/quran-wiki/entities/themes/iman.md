# Iman — Faith and Belief

## Definition

Iman (إِيمَان) is the firm belief in the heart, affirmation by the tongue, and action by the limbs. It is the foundational response to tawhid and the prerequisite for all righteous deeds.

## Etymology

أ-م-ن (amana) — to be secure, safe, tranquil; the root of mu'min (believer) and amanah (trust).

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2:136)]] — 'Say: We believe in Allah and what has been revealed to us'
- [[surah-004-an-nisa|An-Nisa (4:136)]] — 'O you who have believed, believe in Allah and His Messenger and the Book'
- [[surah-049-al-hujurat|Al-Hujurat (49:14)]] — 'The bedouins say: We have believed. Say: You have not believed; but say: We have submitted'
