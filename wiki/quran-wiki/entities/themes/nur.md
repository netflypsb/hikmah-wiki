# Nur — Divine Light

## Overview

**Nur** (نُور) means light. In the Quran, it refers both to physical light and, more profoundly, to divine spiritual light — the guidance of Allah that illuminates hearts and removes the darkness of ignorance and disbelief.

## Key Verses

- [[verses/ayat-an-nur|Ayat an-Nur — 24:35]] — Allah is the Light of the heavens and the earth
- [[surah-024-an-nur|Surah An-Nur]] — named after this attribute
