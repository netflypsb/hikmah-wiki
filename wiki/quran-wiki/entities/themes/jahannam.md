# Jahannam — Hellfire

## Definition

Jahannam (جَهَنَّم) is the abode of punishment for disbelievers, hypocrites, and persistent wrongdoers. It is described with physical torment (fire, boiling water, chains, scorching wind) and spiritual anguish (remorse, shame, divine abandonment).

## Etymology

From the valley of Gehenna near Jerusalem; adopted into Arabic as the proper name of Hell.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-004-an-nisa|An-Nisa (4:56)]] — 'Indeed those who disbelieve in Our signs — We will burn them in a Fire'
- [[surah-067-al-mulk|Al-Mulk (67:6-11)]] — 'For those who disbelieve in their Lord is the punishment of Hell'
- [[surah-111-al-masad|Al-Masad (111)]] — Abu Lahab and his wife in the Fire of blazing flame
