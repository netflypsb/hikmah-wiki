# Risalah — Messengership

## Definition

Risalah (رِسَالَة) is the sending of prophets and messengers to every nation with divine guidance. It affirms that Allah has not left humanity without guidance, and that Muhammad (peace be upon him) is the final messenger (Khatam al-Anbiya).

## Etymology

ر-س-ل (rasala) — to send, dispatch, delegate with authority.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2)]] — 'We have sent you with the truth as a bringer of good tidings and a warner' (2:119)
- [[surah-003-aali-imran|Aali Imran (3)]] — 'Indeed Allah has favoured the believers when He sent among them a messenger from themselves' (3:164)
- [[surah-021-al-anbiya|Al-Anbiya (21)]] — 'We made them leaders guiding by Our command' (21:73)
- [[surah-033-al-ahzab|Al-Ahzab (33)]] — 'Muhammad is not the father of any of your men, but the Messenger of Allah and the seal of the prophets' (33:40)

## Tafsir Perspectives

Tafsir Ibn Kathir on 33:40: 'The phrase Khatam al-Nabiyyin means there will be no prophet after him...' This is the definitive Sunni position on the finality of prophethood.
