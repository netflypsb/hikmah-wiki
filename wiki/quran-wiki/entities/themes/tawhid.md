# Tawhid — Divine Oneness

## Definition

Tawhid (تَوْحِيد) is the absolute oneness of Allah in His Lordship (Rububiyyah), His right to worship (Uluhiyyah), and His Names and Attributes (Asma wa Sifat). It is the central pillar of Islamic faith and the predominant theme of the Qur'an.

## Etymology

و-ح-د (wahada) — to be one, unique, without partner or equal.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-001-al-fatihah|Al-Fatihah (1)]] — 'You alone we worship, You alone we ask for help' (1:5)
- [[surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2)]] — 'Your God is One God' (2:163)
- [[surah-112-al-ikhlas|Al-Ikhlas (112)]] — The surah of pure tawhid: Qul huwa Allahu Ahad (112:1-4)
- [[surah-038-sad|Sad (38)]] — 'Allah, He is One, the Irresistible' (38:65)
- [[surah-039-az-zumar|Az-Zumar (39)]] — 'Allah is the Creator of all things, and He is the Disposer of all affairs' (39:62)

## Tafsir Perspectives

Tafsir Ibn Kathir on 2:163: 'This verse is one of the greatest proofs of tawhid...' Ibn Kathir traces tawhid as the message of every prophet, from Nuh to Muhammad (peace be upon them all).
