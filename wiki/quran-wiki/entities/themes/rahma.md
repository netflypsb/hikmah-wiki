# Rahma — Divine Mercy

## Definition

Rahma (رَحْمَة) is the all-encompassing mercy of Allah — Ar-Rahman (the Lord of all creation) and Ar-Rahim (the Merciful to the believers). It is the opening attribute of the Qur'an and the prevailing theme of Surah Ar-Rahman.

## Etymology

ر-ح-م (rahima) — the womb, compassion, tenderness that encompasses and protects.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-001-al-fatihah|Al-Fatihah (1)]] — 'Bismillah ir-Rahman ir-Rahim' — the first words of the Qur'an
- [[surah-055-ar-rahman|Ar-Rahman (55)]] — 'Which of the favours of your Lord will you deny?' — mercy in creation and reward
- [[surah-006-al-an'am|Al-An'am (6)]] — 'My mercy encompasses all things' (6:12, 6:54)
- [[surah-039-az-zumar|Az-Zumar (39)]] — 'Say: O My servants who have transgressed against themselves, do not despair of the mercy of Allah' (39:53)
