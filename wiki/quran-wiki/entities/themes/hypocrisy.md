# Hypocrisy — Nifaq

## Definition

Hypocrisy (نِفَاق) in the Qur'an is the outward profession of faith while inwardly concealing disbelief or malice. It is worse than open kufr because it deceives the community and undermines trust.

## Etymology

ن-ف-ق (nafaqa) — to exit through a tunnel, to conceal; munafiq as one who enters Islam by one door and exits by another.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2:8-20)]] — The longest description of hypocrites in the Qur'an
- [[surah-063-al-munafiqun|Al-Munafiqun (63)]] — 'When the hypocrites come to you, they say: We testify that you are the Messenger of Allah'
- [[surah-009-at-tawbah|At-Tawbah (9:67)]] — 'The hypocrite men and hypocrite women are of one another'
