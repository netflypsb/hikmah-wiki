# Miracle — Mu'jizah

## Definition

A miracle (مُعْجِزَة) is a supernatural act performed by a prophet at Allah's command, serving as proof of prophethood. Unlike magic (sihr), it cannot be replicated and always affirms truth.

## Etymology

ع-ج-ز (a'jaza) — to render powerless, incapacitate; mu'jizah as that which defeats all challenge.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-003-aali-imran|Aali Imran (3:49)]] — 'And I heal the blind and the leper, and I raise the dead by permission of Allah' — Isa's miracles
- [[surah-007-al-a'raf|Al-A'raf (7:106-108)]] — 'So he threw his staff, and suddenly it was a serpent manifest' — Musa's staff
- [[surah-054-al-qamar|Al-Qamar (54:1-2)]] — 'The Hour has come near, and the moon has split' — the Prophet's greatest miracle
