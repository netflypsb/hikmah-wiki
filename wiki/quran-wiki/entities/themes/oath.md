# Oath — Qasam

## Definition

The divine oath (قَسَم) is Allah's swearing by created things (the sun, moon, stars, dawn, night, pen, etc.) to affirm the truth of what follows. It is a Makki rhetorical device of immense emphasis.

## Etymology

ق-س-م (qasama) — to divide, swear, apportion.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-091-ash-shams|Ash-Shams (91:1-2)]] — 'By the sun and its brightness, and by the moon when it follows it'
- [[surah-095-at-tin|At-Tin (95:1-3)]] — 'By the fig and the olive, and by Mount Sinai'
- [[surah-056-al-waqi'a|Al-Waqi'a (56:75-76)]] — 'I swear by the setting of the stars — and indeed it is an oath, if you knew, tremendous'
