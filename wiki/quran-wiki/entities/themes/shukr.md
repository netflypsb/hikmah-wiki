# Shukr — Gratitude

## Definition

Shukr (شُكْر) is gratitude, thankfulness, and the recognition of blessings. It is paired with sabr (patience) as the two wings of the believer.

## Etymology

ش-ك-ر (shakara) — to be grateful, to acknowledge; the root of shakir (grateful).

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2:152)]] — 'Then remember Me; I will remember you. And be grateful to Me'
- [[surah-014-ibrahim|Ibrahim (14:7)]] — 'If you are grateful, I will surely increase you'
- [[surah-031-luqman|Luqman (31:12)]] — 'And We had certainly given Luqman wisdom: Be grateful to Allah'
