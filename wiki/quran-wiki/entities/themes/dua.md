# Dua — Supplication

## Definition

Dua (دُعَاء) is the calling upon Allah in need, praise, or request. It is the essence of worship ('dua is the marrow of worship' — hadith) and the primary means by which the servant draws near to the Lord.

## Etymology

د-ع-و (da'aa) — to call, summon, invite; dua as the invocation of the humble.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2:186)]] — 'And when My servants ask you concerning Me — indeed I am near. I respond to the invocation of the supplicant when he calls upon Me'
- [[surah-040-ghafir|Ghafir (40:60)]] — 'Call upon Me; I will respond to you'
- [[surah-001-al-fatihah|Al-Fatihah (1:5-7)]] — 'You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help' — the supreme dua
