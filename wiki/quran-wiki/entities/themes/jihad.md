# Jihad — Striving in Allah's Cause

## Definition

Jihad (جِهَاد) is striving, struggling, and exerting effort in the path of Allah. It encompasses spiritual struggle against the soul (jihad al-nafs), intellectual defense of Islam (jihad al-lisan), and military defense when attacked (jihad al-sayf).

## Etymology

ج-ه-د (jahada) — to strive, exert, struggle, labor.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2:190-193)]] — 'Fight in the path of Allah those who fight you'
- [[surah-009-at-tawbah|At-Tawbah (9:111)]] — 'Allah has purchased from the believers their lives and their properties'
- [[surah-029-al-ankabut|Al-Ankabut (29:69)]] — 'And those who strive for Us — We will surely guide them to Our ways'
