# Command — Amr

## Definition

Command (أَمْر) in the Qur'an is the divine imperative — Allah's ordering of His servants toward belief, worship, righteousness, and obedience. It is the primary mode of divine-human communication in legislation.

## Etymology

أ-م-ر (amara) — to command, order, decree, appoint.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2:21)]] — 'O mankind, worship your Lord'
- [[surah-073-al-muzzammil|Al-Muzzammil (73:2-4)]] — 'Stand in prayer by night, all but a small part of it'
- [[surah-096-al-alaq|Al-'Alaq (96:1)]] — 'Read in the name of your Lord who created' — the first command
