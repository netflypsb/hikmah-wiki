# Adab — Etiquette and Good Conduct

## Definition

Adab (أَدَب) in the Qur'an is proper conduct, courtesy, and moral refinement. It encompasses etiquette toward Allah, the Prophet, parents, neighbors, and the community.

## Etymology

أ-د-ب (adaba) — to invite, gather, discipline; adab as the discipline of the soul toward excellence.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-004-an-nisa|An-Nisa (4:86)]] — 'And when you are greeted with a greeting, greet in return with what is better'
- [[surah-024-an-nur|An-Nur (24:27)]] — 'O you who have believed, do not enter houses other than your own houses until you ascertain welcome'
- [[surah-049-al-hujurat|Al-Hujurat (49:11-12)]] — 'O you who have believed, let not a people ridicule another people'
