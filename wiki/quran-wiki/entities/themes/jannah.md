# Jannah — Paradise

## Definition

Jannah (جَنَّة) is the eternal garden of delight for the believers, with rivers of pure water, milk, wine, and honey; couches, gardens, fruits, and the supreme pleasure: the vision of Allah (ru'yatullah).

## Etymology

ج-ن-ن (janna) — to cover, conceal, protect; the garden as a hidden sanctuary.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2:25)]] — 'Gardens beneath which rivers flow'
- [[surah-055-ar-rahman|Ar-Rahman (55:46-77)]] — Two gardens with every kind of fruit in pairs
- [[surah-076-al-insan|Al-Insan (76:5-22)]] — The righteous drink from a cup whose mixture is of camphor and sealed nectar
