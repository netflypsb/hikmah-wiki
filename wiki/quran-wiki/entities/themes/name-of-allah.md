# Names of Allah — Asma ul-Husna

## Definition

The names and attributes of Allah (أَسْمَاءُ ٱللَّٰهِ الْحُسْنَىٰ) describe His essence, actions, and relationships with creation. The Qur'an mentions approximately 99 names, each revealing a dimension of divine perfection.

## Etymology

س-م-و (sama) — to be high, elevated; ism as the name that identifies and elevates.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-001-al-fatihah|Al-Fatihah (1:1)]] — 'Bismillah ir-Rahman ir-Rahim' — the opening names
- [[surah-017-al-isra|Al-Isra (17:110)]] — 'Call upon Allah or call upon Ar-Rahman — whichever you call, to Him belong the best names'
- [[surah-020-ta-ha|Ta-Ha (20:8)]] — 'Allah — there is no deity except Him. To Him belong the best names'
