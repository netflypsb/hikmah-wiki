# Creation — Khalq

## Definition

Divine creation (خَلْق) is the origination of all things from non-existence by Allah's will and power. The Qur'an repeatedly points to creation as proof of tawhid, resurrection, and Allah's infinite knowledge.

## Etymology

خ-ل-ق (khalaqa) — to create, measure, proportion, originate; khalq as the measured, proportioned creation.

## Qur'anic Basis

- [[surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2:21)]] — 'O mankind, worship your Lord, who created you and those before you'
- [[surah-032-as-sajda|As-Sajda (32:7)]] — 'He who has made everything which He has created in the best form'
- [[surah-096-al-alaq|Al-'Alaq (96:1-2)]] — 'Read in the name of your Lord who created — created man from a clinging substance'
