# القلم — Al-Qalam (68)

> Al-Qalam — 52 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Al-Qalam (القلم) is the 68th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **52 verses**, spanning **juz 29** (pages 564–566 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **2 rukus**.
