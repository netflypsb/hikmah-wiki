# القصص — Al-Qasas (28)

> Al-Qasas — 88 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Al-Qasas (القصص) is the 28th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **88 verses**, spanning **juz 20** (pages 385–396 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **9 rukus**.

## Tafsir Summary

### Ayah 1

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Makkah Imam Ahmad bin Hanbal, may Allah have mercy on him, recorded that Ma`diykarib said: "We came to `Abdullah and asked him to recite to us: طسم (Ta Sin Mim.) the two hundred. He said, `I do not know it; you should go to someone who learned it from the Messenger of Allah ﷺ Khabbab bin Al-Aratt.' So we went to Khabbab bin Al-Aratt and he recited it to us, may Allah be …

**Muyassar:** (طسم) سبق الكلام على الحروف المقطَّعة في أول سورة البقرة.

### Ayah 2

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Makkah Imam Ahmad bin Hanbal, may Allah have mercy on him, recorded that Ma`diykarib said: "We came to `Abdullah and asked him to recite to us: طسم (Ta Sin Mim.) the two hundred. He said, `I do not know it; you should go to someone who learned it from the Messenger of Allah ﷺ Khabbab bin Al-Aratt.' So we went to Khabbab bin Al-Aratt and he recited it to us, may Allah be …

**Muyassar:** هذه آيات القرآن الذي أنزلته إليك -أيها الرسول-، مبينًا لكل ما يحتاج إليه العباد في دنياهم وأخراهم.

### Ayah 3

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Makkah Imam Ahmad bin Hanbal, may Allah have mercy on him, recorded that Ma`diykarib said: "We came to `Abdullah and asked him to recite to us: طسم (Ta Sin Mim.) the two hundred. He said, `I do not know it; you should go to someone who learned it from the Messenger of Allah ﷺ Khabbab bin Al-Aratt.' So we went to Khabbab bin Al-Aratt and he recited it to us, may Allah be …

**Muyassar:** نقصُّ عليك من خبر موسى وفرعون بالصدق لقوم يؤمنون بهذا القرآن، ويصدِّقون بأنه من عند الله، ويعملون بهديه.
