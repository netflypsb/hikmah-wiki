# السجدة — As-Sajdah (32)

> As-Sajdah — 30 verses, Meccan

## Overview

As-Sajdah (السجدة) is the 32th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **30 verses**, spanning **juz 21** (pages 415–417 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **3 rukus**.

## Tafsir Summary

### Ayah 1

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Al-Madinah The Virtues of Surah Alif Lam Mim As-Sajdah In the Book of the Friday prayer, Al-Bukhari recorded that Abu Hurayrah said, "The Prophet used to recite in Fajr on Fridays: الم تَنزِيلَ (Alif Lam Mim. The revelation of...), As-Sajdah, and هَلْ أَتَى عَلَى الإِنسَـنِ (Has there not been over man...)Al-Insan (76)." This was also recorded by Muslim. Imam Ahmad recorded …

**Muyassar:** (الم) سبق الكلام على الحروف المقطَّعة في أول سورة البقرة.

### Ayah 2

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Al-Madinah The Virtues of Surah Alif Lam Mim As-Sajdah In the Book of the Friday prayer, Al-Bukhari recorded that Abu Hurayrah said, "The Prophet used to recite in Fajr on Fridays: الم تَنزِيلَ (Alif Lam Mim. The revelation of...), As-Sajdah, and هَلْ أَتَى عَلَى الإِنسَـنِ (Has there not been over man...)Al-Insan (76)." This was also recorded by Muslim. Imam Ahmad recorded …

**Muyassar:** هذا القرآن الذي جاء به محمد صلى الله عليه وسلم لا شك أنه منزل من عند الله، رب الخلائق أجمعين.

### Ayah 3

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Al-Madinah The Virtues of Surah Alif Lam Mim As-Sajdah In the Book of the Friday prayer, Al-Bukhari recorded that Abu Hurayrah said, "The Prophet used to recite in Fajr on Fridays: الم تَنزِيلَ (Alif Lam Mim. The revelation of...), As-Sajdah, and هَلْ أَتَى عَلَى الإِنسَـنِ (Has there not been over man...)Al-Insan (76)." This was also recorded by Muslim. Imam Ahmad recorded …

**Muyassar:** بل أيقول المشركون: اختلق محمد صلى الله عليه وسلم القرآن؟ كذَبوا، بل هو الحق الثابت المنزل عليك -أيها الرسول- من ربك; لتنذر به أناسًا لم يأتهم نذير من قبلك، لعلهم يهتدون، فيعرفوا الحق ويؤمنوا به ويؤثروه، ويؤمنوا بك.
