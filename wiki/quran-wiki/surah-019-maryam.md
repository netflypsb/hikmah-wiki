# مريم — Maryam (19)

> Maryam — 98 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Maryam (مريم) is the 19th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **98 verses**, spanning **juz 16** (pages 305–312 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **6 rukus**.

## Tafsir Summary

### Ayah 1

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Makkah Muhammad bin Ishaq recorded a Hadith of Umm Salamah in his Sirah, and Ahmad bin Hanbal recorded from Ibn Mas`ud, the story of the Hijrah (migration) to Ethiopia from Makkah. The narration mentions that Ja`far bin Abi Talib recited the first part of this Surah to An-Najashi and his companions. بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَـنِ الرَّحِيمِ In the Name of Allah, the Most …

**Muyassar:** {كهيعص} سبق الكلام على الحروف المقطَّعة في أول سورة البقرة.
