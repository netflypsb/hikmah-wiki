# الإخلاص — Al-Ikhlas (112)

> Al-Ikhlas — 4 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Al-Ikhlas (الإخلاص) is the 112th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **4 verses**, spanning **juz 30** (pages 604–604 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **1 rukus**.
