# فصلت — Fussilat (41)

> Fussilat — 54 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Fussilat (فصلت) is the 41th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **54 verses**, spanning **juz 24, 25** (pages 477–482 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **6 rukus**.

## Tafsir Summary

### Ayah 1

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Makkah بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَـنِ الرَّحِيمِ In the Name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Description of the Qur'an, and what Those Who turn away from it say. حـم - تَنزِيلٌ مِّنَ الرَّحْمَنِ الرَّحِيمِ (Ha Mim. A revelation from the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.) means, the Qur'an is revealed from the Most Gracious, Most Merciful. This is like the Ayat: …

**Muyassar:** {حم} سبق الكلام على الحروف المقطَّعة في أول سورة البقرة.

Description of the Qur'an, and what Those Who turn away from it say. حـم - تَنزِيلٌ مِّنَ الرَّحْمَنِ الرَّحِيمِ (Ha Mim. A revelation from the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.) means, the Qur'an is revealed from the Most Gracious, Most Merciful. This is like the Ayat: …

**Muyassar:** هذا القرآن الكريم تنزيل من الرحمن الرحيم، نزَّله على نبيه محمد صلى الله عليه وسلم.
