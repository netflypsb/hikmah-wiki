# المؤمنون — Al-Mu'minun (23)

> Al-Mu'minun — 118 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Al-Mu'minun (المؤمنون) is the 23th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **118 verses**, spanning **juz 18** (pages 342–349 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **6 rukus**.

## Tafsir Summary

### Ayah 1

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was Revealed in Makkah بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَـنِ الرَّحِيمِ In the Name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. The Success is for the believers whose qualities are described here قَدْ أَفْلَحَ الْمُؤْمِنُونَ (Successful indeed are the believers) means, they have attained victory and are blessed, for they have succeeded. These are the believers who have the following …

**Muyassar:** قد فاز المصدِّقون بالله وبرسوله العاملون بشرعه.

### Ayah 2

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was Revealed in Makkah بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَـنِ الرَّحِيمِ In the Name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. The Success is for the believers whose qualities are described here قَدْ أَفْلَحَ الْمُؤْمِنُونَ (Successful indeed are the believers) means, they have attained victory and are blessed, for they have succeeded. These are the believers who have the following …

**Muyassar:** الذين من صفاتهم أنهم في صلاتهم خاشعون، تَفْرُغُ لها قلوبهم، وتسكن جوارحهم.

### Ayah 3

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was Revealed in Makkah بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَـنِ الرَّحِيمِ In the Name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. The Success is for the believers whose qualities are described here قَدْ أَفْلَحَ الْمُؤْمِنُونَ (Successful indeed are the believers) means, they have attained victory and are blessed, for they have succeeded. These are the believers who have the following …

**Muyassar:** والذين هم تاركون لكل ما لا خير فيه من الأقوال والأفعال.
