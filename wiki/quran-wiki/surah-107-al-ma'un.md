# الماعون — Al-Ma'un (107)

> Al-Ma'un — 7 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Al-Ma'un (الماعون) is the 107th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **7 verses**, spanning **juz 30** (pages 602–602 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **1 rukus**.

## Tafsir Summary

### Ayah 1

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Makkah بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَـنِ الرَّحِيمِ In the Name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Allah says, "O Muhammad! Have you seen the one who denies the Din" Here the word Din means the Hereafter, the Recompense and the Final Reward. فَذَلِكَ الَّذِى يَدُعُّ الْيَتِيمَ (That is he who repulses the orphan, ) meaning, he is the one who oppresses the orphan and …

**Muyassar:** أرأيت حال ذلك الذي يكذِّب بالبعث والجزاء؟

### Ayah 2

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Makkah بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَـنِ الرَّحِيمِ In the Name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Allah says, "O Muhammad! Have you seen the one who denies the Din" Here the word Din means the Hereafter, the Recompense and the Final Reward. فَذَلِكَ الَّذِى يَدُعُّ الْيَتِيمَ (That is he who repulses the orphan, ) meaning, he is the one who oppresses the orphan and …

**Muyassar:** فذلك الذي يدفع اليتيم الذي مات أبوه وهو صغير بعنف وشدة عن حقه؛ لقساوة قلبه.

### Ayah 3

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Makkah بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَـنِ الرَّحِيمِ In the Name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Allah says, "O Muhammad! Have you seen the one who denies the Din" Here the word Din means the Hereafter, the Recompense and the Final Reward. فَذَلِكَ الَّذِى يَدُعُّ الْيَتِيمَ (That is he who repulses the orphan, ) meaning, he is the one who oppresses the orphan and …

**Muyassar:** ولا يحضُّ غيره على إطعام المحتاج الذي لا يملك ما يكفيه ويسد حاجته، فكيف له أن يطعمه بنفسه؟
