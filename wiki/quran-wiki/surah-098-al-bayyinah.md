# البينة — Al-Bayyinah (98)

> Al-Bayyinah — 8 verses, Medinan

## Overview

Al-Bayyinah (البينة) is the 98th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Medinan** surah comprising **8 verses**, spanning **juz 30** (pages 598–599 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **1 rukus**.

## Tafsir Summary

### Ayah 1

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Madina The Messenger of Allah recited this Surah to Ubayy Imam Ahmad recorded from Anas bin Malik that the Messenger of Allah said to Ubayy bin Ka`b, «إِنَّ اللهَ أَمَرَنِي أَنْ أَقْرَأَ عَلَيْكَ» لَمْ يَكُنِ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِنْ أَهْلِ الْكِتَـبِ (Verily, Allah has commanded me to recite to you (Those who disbelieve from among the People of the Scripture.)) Ubayy said, …

**Muyassar:** لم يكن الذين كفروا من اليهود والنصارى والمشركين تاركين كفرهم حتى تأتيهم العلامة التي وُعِدوا بها في الكتب السابقة.

### Ayah 2

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Madina The Messenger of Allah recited this Surah to Ubayy Imam Ahmad recorded from Anas bin Malik that the Messenger of Allah said to Ubayy bin Ka`b, «إِنَّ اللهَ أَمَرَنِي أَنْ أَقْرَأَ عَلَيْكَ» لَمْ يَكُنِ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِنْ أَهْلِ الْكِتَـبِ (Verily, Allah has commanded me to recite to you (Those who disbelieve from among the People of the Scripture.)) Ubayy said, …

**Muyassar:** وهي رسول الله محمد صلى الله عليه وسلم، يتلو قرآنًا في صحف مطهرة.

### Ayah 3

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Madina The Messenger of Allah recited this Surah to Ubayy Imam Ahmad recorded from Anas bin Malik that the Messenger of Allah said to Ubayy bin Ka`b, «إِنَّ اللهَ أَمَرَنِي أَنْ أَقْرَأَ عَلَيْكَ» لَمْ يَكُنِ الَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ مِنْ أَهْلِ الْكِتَـبِ (Verily, Allah has commanded me to recite to you (Those who disbelieve from among the People of the Scripture.)) Ubayy said, …

**Muyassar:** في تلك الصحف أخبار صادقة وأوامر عادلة، تهدي إلى الحق وإلى صراط مستقيم.
