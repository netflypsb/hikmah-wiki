# Amal (عَمَل)

## Meaning

Amal means **Action / Deed**

## Root Analysis

From the root a-m-l, meaning to do, work, or labor. Amal in the Quran is inseparable from iman (faith): 'It is not your wishes nor the wishes of the People of the Book: whoever does evil will be recompensed for it' (4:123). Every amal is 'written' and 'weighed' on the Day of Judgment.
