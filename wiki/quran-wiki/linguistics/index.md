# Linguistics — Arabic Word Studies

## Overview

The Quran was revealed in Arabic — a language of astonishing precision, depth, and semantic range. Each Arabic root carries a cluster of meanings that unfold across contexts, revealing layers of connection and coherence. These word studies explore the etymology, semantic field, and Quranic usage of key Arabic terms.
