# Jahannam (جَهَنَّم)

## Meaning

Jahannam means **Hellfire**

## Root Analysis

From the Hebrew Gehinnom (Valley of Hinnom). In the Quran, it is the ultimate abode of the wicked, described with graphic intensity: chains, boiling water, scorching wind, and darkness. Yet even in descriptions of Jahannam, the Quran offers hope: 'Those who say: Our Lord, indeed we have believed, so forgive us our sins and protect us from the punishment of the Fire' (3:16).
