# Jannah (جَنَّة)

## Meaning

Jannah means **Garden / Paradise**

## Root Analysis

From the root j-n-n, meaning to conceal or cover. Jannah is a garden hidden and protected — a place of concealment from hardship. Its etymology suggests a sheltered, verdant space, and the Quran describes it with layers of gardens beneath which rivers flow, eternal shade, and perfected companionship.
