# Huda (هُدًى)

## Meaning

Huda means **Guidance**

## Root Analysis

From the root h-d-y, meaning to guide, show the way, or send toward a destination. Huda is not merely information — it is the active guidance of the heart toward truth. The Quran is called 'al-huda' (the guidance), and those who reject it are described as having lost the capacity to be guided.
