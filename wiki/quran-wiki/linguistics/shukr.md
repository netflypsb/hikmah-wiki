# Shukr (شُكْر)

## Meaning

Shukr means **Gratitude / Thankfulness**

## Root Analysis

From the root sh-k-r, meaning to acknowledge a favor and to increase it. Shukr is not mere verbal thanks but active recognition through the limbs — using blessings in obedience to Allah. Its opposite is kufr, which means both disbelief and ingratitude.
