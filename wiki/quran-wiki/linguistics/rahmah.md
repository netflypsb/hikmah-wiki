# Rahmah (رَحْمَة)

## Meaning

Rahmah means **Mercy / Womb**

## Root Analysis

From the root r-h-m, the same root as 'womb' (rahm). The Quran explicitly links divine mercy to the maternal bond: Allah says His mercy encompasses all things. The Prophet (SAW) received the hadith: 'Allah created mercy in one hundred parts — ninety-nine are reserved for the Day of Judgment, one part is what creation shows each other, from which all mercy derives.'
