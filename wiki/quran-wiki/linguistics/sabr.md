# Sabr (صَبْر)

## Meaning

Sabr means **Patience / Perseverance**

## Root Analysis

From the root s-b-r, originally meaning to bind or restrain. Sabr in the Quran is threefold: patience in obedience, patience in avoiding sin, and patience in affliction. It is often paired with salah (prayer): 'Seek help in patience and prayer' (2:153).
