# Taqwa (تَّقْوَى)

## Meaning

Taqwa means **God-consciousness / Piety**

## Root Analysis

From the root w-q-y, meaning to guard or protect. Taqwa is the act of guarding oneself against what displeases Allah. It is frequently paired with iman (faith), and the Quran states: 'The most honored of you in the sight of Allah is the one with the most taqwa' (49:13).
