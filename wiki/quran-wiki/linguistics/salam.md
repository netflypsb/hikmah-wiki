# Salam (سَلَام)

## Meaning

Salam means **Peace / Submission**

## Root Analysis

The root s-l-m encompasses safety, soundness, greeting, and the act of submitting. It names the religion itself (Islam) and its adherents (Muslim). The Quran uses salam as the greeting of paradise and the word spoken by angels to the righteous.
