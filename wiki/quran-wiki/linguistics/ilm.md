# Ilm (عِلْم)

## Meaning

Ilm means **Knowledge**

## Root Analysis

From the root a-l-m, meaning to know, to mark, or to teach. Ilm in the Quran encompasses both revealed knowledge and acquired knowledge. The very first word revealed was 'Read!' (96:1), establishing knowledge as the foundation of the prophetic mission. Allah teaches by the pen what humanity did not know.
