# Nur (نُور)

## Meaning

Nur means **Light**

## Root Analysis

The root n-w-r denotes light, illumination, and manifestation. In the Quran, nur is both physical light and divine guidance — the light of faith that illuminates the heart. The famous 'Ayat an-Nur' (24:35) describes Allah as 'the Light of the heavens and the earth.'
