# Qawm (قَوْم)

## Meaning

Qawm means **People / Nation**

## Root Analysis

From the root q-w-m, meaning to stand upright. A qawm is a people who stand together — by shared lineage, faith, or moral orientation. The Quran repeatedly addresses the 'qawm' of each prophet: O my people, return to Allah.
