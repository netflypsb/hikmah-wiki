# Ummah (أُمَّة)

## Meaning

Ummah means **Community / Nation**

## Root Analysis

From the same root as 'umm' (mother). An ummah is a community bound by shared values and purpose. The Quran uses it for all communities who received prophets (every ummah had a messenger) and for the Muslim ummah as the culmination of that prophetic chain.
