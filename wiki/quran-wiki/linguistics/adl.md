# Adl (عَدْل)

## Meaning

Adl means **Justice / Equity**

## Root Analysis

From the root a-d-l, meaning to be equal, straight, or balanced. Adl is one of Allah's names (Al-Adl) and a central moral command. The Quran commands believers to 'stand firm for justice, even if it is against yourselves, your parents, or your relatives' (4:135).
