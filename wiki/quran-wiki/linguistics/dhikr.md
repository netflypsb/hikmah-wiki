# Dhikr (ذِكْر)

## Meaning

Dhikr means **Remembrance / Mention**

## Root Analysis

From the root dh-k-r, meaning to remember, mention, or invoke. Dhikr is the conscious act of remembering Allah — with the heart, tongue, and limbs. The Quran commands: 'Remember Me, and I will remember you' (2:152). Those who remember Allah standing, sitting, and lying down are distinguished in the sight of their Lord.
