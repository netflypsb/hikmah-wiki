# الأحقاف — Al-Ahqaf (46)

> Al-Ahqaf — 35 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Al-Ahqaf (الأحقاف) is the 46th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **35 verses**, spanning **juz 26** (pages 502–506 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **4 rukus**.

## Tafsir Summary

### Ayah 1

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Makkah بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَـنِ الرَّحِيمِ In the Name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. The Qur'an is a Revelation from Allah and the Universe is His True Creation Allah informs that He has revealed the Book to His servant and Messenger Muhammad -- may Allah's blessings be upon him until the Day of Judgement. Allah then describes Himself as being of …

**Muyassar:** {حم} سبق الكلام على الحروف المقطَّعة في أول سورة البقرة.

### Ayah 4

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Makkah بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَـنِ الرَّحِيمِ In the Name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. The Qur'an is a Revelation from Allah and the Universe is His True Creation Allah informs that He has revealed the Book to His servant and Messenger Muhammad -- may Allah's blessings be upon him until the Day of Judgement. Allah then describes Himself as being of …

**Muyassar:** قل -أيها الرسول- لهؤلاء الكفار: أرأيتم الآلهة، والأوثان التي تعبدونها من دون الله، أروني أيَّ شيء خلقوا من الأرض، أم لهم مع الله نصيب من خلق السموات؟ ائتوني بكتاب من عند الله من قبل هذا القرآن أو ببقيَّة من علم، إن كنتم صادقين فيما تزعمون.
