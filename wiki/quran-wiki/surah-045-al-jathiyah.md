# الجاثية — Al-Jathiyah (45)

> Al-Jathiyah — 37 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Al-Jathiyah (الجاثية) is the 45th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **37 verses**, spanning **juz 25** (pages 499–502 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **4 rukus**.
