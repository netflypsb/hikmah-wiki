# Taqdeem wa al-Ta'kheer

## Arabic

**التَّقْدِيم وَالتَّأْخِير**

## Meaning

Taqdeem wa al-Ta'kheer means **Hysteron proteron — placing the expected later element first and the earlier element later, to create emphasis or emotional impact.**

## Definition

Taqdeem wa al-Ta'kheer (التقديم والتأخير) is the deliberate reversal of the natural or chronological order of elements in a sentence. The Quran often places the consequence before the cause, the answer before the question, or the result before the action to create dramatic tension, emphasize the outcome, or mirror the psychological experience of the listener.
