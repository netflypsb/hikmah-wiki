# Haal

## Arabic

**الْحَال**

## Meaning

Haal means **Circumstantial clause — describing a simultaneous state or condition, often painting a vivid scene in a single phrase.**

## Definition

Haal (الحال) is the grammatical construction that describes the state or condition of the subject while performing an action. In the Quran, it often creates cinematic imagery: the disbelievers 'going about it, distracted in mind' (50:3), or the angels entering upon the family of Ibrahim 'with peace' (51:25).
