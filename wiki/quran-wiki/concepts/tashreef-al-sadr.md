# Tashreef al-Sadr (Expansion of the Breast)

## Arabic

**تَشْرِيفُ الصَّدْر**

## Meaning

Tashreef al-Sadr (Expansion of the Breast) means **The metaphor of 'expansion of the breast' — a Quranic idiom for tranquility, certainty, and readiness to bear responsibility.**

## Definition

Tashreef al-Sadr (تشريف الصدر) is one of the most powerful Quranic metaphors for inner transformation. When Allah says He 'expanded the breast' of His Messenger (20:25), He describes the removal of constriction — anxiety, doubt, narrowness of heart — and its replacement with openness, clarity, and capacity. It is both a rhetorical image and a theological principle: the heart is the locus of faith, and its expansion is Allah's gift to the sincere.
