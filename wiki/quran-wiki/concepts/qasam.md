# Qasam (Oath)

## Arabic

**القَسَم**

## Meaning

Qasam (Oath) means **Oath — swearing by Allah's creation to emphasize the weight of what follows.**

## Definition

Qasam (القسم) is the rhetorical device of swearing by creation (the sun, the moon, the dawn, the night, the fig, the olive, etc.) to draw attention to a profound truth. The Quran frequently uses oaths before stating a principle, a warning, or a promise. The response to the oath (jawab al-qasam) is often theologically weighty, shifting the focus from the created thing to the Creator.
