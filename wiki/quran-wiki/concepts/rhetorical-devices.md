# Rhetorical Devices — Balagha in the Quran

## Overview

The Quran is not merely a book of legislation or theology — it is a literary masterpiece of unparalleled rhetorical power. The science of **balagha** (eloquence) studies the devices by which the Quran achieves its effect. These devices are not decorative flourishes; they are the bones and sinews of meaning.

## Catalog of Devices

- [[concepts/iltifat|Iltifat]] — الْإِلْتِفَات — Abrupt grammatical shift in pronoun, person, number, or tense mid-verse — a hallmark of Quranic rhetoric that redirects attention and magnifies meaning.
- [[concepts/tashbeeh|Tashbeeh]] — التَّشْبِيه — Simile — explicit comparison using tools of similitude such as 'ka' (كَ, 'like') or 'mithl' (مِثْل, 'as').
- [[concepts/qasam|Qasam (Oath)]] — القَسَم — Oath — swearing by Allah's creation to emphasize the weight of what follows.
- [[concepts/taqdeem-wa-takheer|Taqdeem wa al-Ta'kheer]] — التَّقْدِيم وَالتَّأْخِير — Hysteron proteron — placing the expected later element first and the earlier element later, to create emphasis or emotional impact.
- [[concepts/haal|Haal]] — الْحَال — Circumstantial clause — describing a simultaneous state or condition, often painting a vivid scene in a single phrase.
- [[concepts/tamtheel|Tamtheel (Parable)]] — الْتَّمْثِيل — Extended simile or parable — a self-contained narrative comparison that illustrates a spiritual or moral principle.
- [[concepts/muzawaja|Muzawaja (Antithesis)]] — الْمُزَاوَجَة — Pairing or antithesis — placing opposites side by side to highlight contrast and create symmetry.
- [[concepts/ijaaz|I'jaaz (Inimitability)]] — الْإِعْجَاز — The miraculous inimitability of the Quran — rhetorical, linguistic, scientific, legislative, and prophetic.
- [[concepts/jawab-al-qasam|Jawab al-Qasam]] — جَوَابُ الْقَسَم — The response to an oath — the truth being affirmed, which gives the oath its purpose and weight.
- [[concepts/kinayah|Kinayah]] — الْكِنَايَة — Figurative expression or metonymy — using a related term to avoid bluntness, add dignity, or convey deeper meaning.
- [[concepts/saj|Saj' (Rhymed Prose)]] — السَّجْع — Rhymed, rhythmic prose — the Quran's distinctive cadence that is neither poetry nor ordinary prose, but a unique literary form.
- [[concepts/tashreef-al-sadr|Tashreef al-Sadr (Expansion of the Breast)]] — تَشْرِيفُ الصَّدْر — The metaphor of 'expansion of the breast' — a Quranic idiom for tranquility, certainty, and readiness to bear responsibility.
