# Muzawaja (Antithesis)

## Arabic

**الْمُزَاوَجَة**

## Meaning

Muzawaja (Antithesis) means **Pairing or antithesis — placing opposites side by side to highlight contrast and create symmetry.**

## Definition

Muzawaja (المزاوجة) is the rhetorical pairing of opposites: jahannam / jannah, kufr / iman, darkness / light, guidance / misguidance. By placing these pairs in parallel structures, the Quran magnifies the distance between them and forces the listener to choose. The contrast is not merely stylistic — it is theological and existential.
