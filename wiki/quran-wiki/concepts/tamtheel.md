# Tamtheel (Parable)

## Arabic

**الْتَّمْثِيل**

## Meaning

Tamtheel (Parable) means **Extended simile or parable — a self-contained narrative comparison that illustrates a spiritual or moral principle.**

## Definition

Tamtheel (التمثيل) goes beyond tashbeeh (simile) by constructing a full narrative scenario. The Quran is rich in parables: the good word is like a good tree, the bad word like a rotten tree (14:24-26); the hypocrites are like a man who kindled a fire (2:17). These parables are not mere illustrations — they are invitations to transformation.
