# Asbab al-Nuzul — Occasions of Revelation

## Definition

Asbab al-Nuzul (أَسْبَابُ ٱلنُّزُول) are the historical contexts, events, and questions that occasioned the revelation of specific verses or passages. While every verse is eternal, understanding its immediate context is essential for correct exegesis (tafsir).

## Etymology

**Sabab** (سَبَب) — cause, reason, means, connection. **Nuzul** (نُزُول) — descent, revelation. Literally: 'the reasons of the descent.'

## Detailed Explanation

**Classical approach:** Imam al-Wahidi (d. 468 AH) compiled the first dedicated work; later expanded by al-Suyuti. Each sabab is traced through authenticated chains (isnad).

**Scope:** Not every verse has a recorded sabab. Some are general; some have multiple asbab for different verses within one surah.

**Usage in tafsir:** Asbab clarify ambiguous verses, establish legal rulings (when the occasion is a specific case or general principle), and resolve apparent contradictions.

**Examples:**
- [[surah-002-al-baqarah|Al-Baqarah (2:256)]] — 'There is no compulsion in religion' — revealed regarding Ansari families whose children were held by Jewish tribes in Khaybar
- [[surah-004-an-nisa|An-Nisa (4:34)]] — 'Men are qawwamun over women' — revealed regarding a woman who complained to the Prophet about her husband's neglect
- [[surah-024-an-nur|An-Nur (24:11-20)]] — The ifk (slander against Aisha RA), establishing the hadd of qadhf
- [[surah-073-al-muzzammil|Al-Muzzammil (73:1-8)]] — 'Stand in prayer by night' — later abrogated by 73:20

## Tafsir Perspectives

Tafsir Ibn Kathir: 'Knowing the occasion of revelation is one of the greatest aids to understanding the meaning... for the sabab is the eye of the tafsir.' The sabab specifies the context, not the limit — a verse revealed about a specific incident may have general application (classical usul al-fiqh).
