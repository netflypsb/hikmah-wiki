# Iltifat

## Arabic

**الْإِلْتِفَات**

## Meaning

Iltifat means **Abrupt grammatical shift in pronoun, person, number, or tense mid-verse — a hallmark of Quranic rhetoric that redirects attention and magnifies meaning.**

## Definition

Iltifat (الإلتفات) literally means 'turning away.' In Quranic rhetoric, it is the sudden shift from one grammatical form to another — for example, from addressing the Prophet in the singular to addressing the believers in the plural, or from third person to second person. This rhetorical shift is never arbitrary; it signals a change in tone, emphasis, or the intended audience.
