# Kinayah

## Arabic

**الْكِنَايَة**

## Meaning

Kinayah means **Figurative expression or metonymy — using a related term to avoid bluntness, add dignity, or convey deeper meaning.**

## Definition

Kinayah (الكناية) is the use of indirect or figurative language to express something that might otherwise be stated directly. In the Quran, kinayah appears in descriptions of intimacy, death, divine anger, or human failing — preserving the dignity of the subject while conveying the meaning unmistakably. Examples include 'he approached his wife' for conjugal relations, and 'their hearts are between their hands' for sincerity.
