# Jawab al-Qasam

## Arabic

**جَوَابُ الْقَسَم**

## Meaning

Jawab al-Qasam means **The response to an oath — the truth being affirmed, which gives the oath its purpose and weight.**

## Definition

Jawab al-Qasam (جواب القسم) is the clause that answers or completes the oath. An oath in the Quran is never made in vain; the jawab delivers the theological or moral payload. When Allah swears by the dawn, the answer is: 'Indeed, the recitation of the dawn is ever witnessed' (17:78). The oath draws the ear; the jawab delivers the meaning.
