# Tashbeeh

## Arabic

**التَّشْبِيه**

## Meaning

Tashbeeh means **Simile — explicit comparison using tools of similitude such as 'ka' (كَ, 'like') or 'mithl' (مِثْل, 'as').**

## Definition

Tashbeeh (التشبيه) is the explicit likening of one thing to another to highlight an attribute or deepen understanding. The Quran uses similes that are precise, evocative, and often challenge the listener's imagination: the unbelievers are 'like cattle, nay more misguided' (7:179), and the righteous are 'like a garden on a height' (2:265).
