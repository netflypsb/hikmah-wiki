# I'jaaz (Inimitability)

## Arabic

**الْإِعْجَاز**

## Meaning

I'jaaz (Inimitability) means **The miraculous inimitability of the Quran — rhetorical, linguistic, scientific, legislative, and prophetic.**

## Definition

I'jaaz (الإعجاز) is the doctrine that the Quran cannot be reproduced or matched in style, content, or structure — a challenge issued to all of creation. While not a single device, the concept of i'jaaz encompasses every rhetorical feature of the Quran: its conciseness with breadth, its rhythm with precision, its predictions with accuracy. It is the overarching banner under which all balagha operates.
