# Saj' (Rhymed Prose)

## Arabic

**السَّجْع**

## Meaning

Saj' (Rhymed Prose) means **Rhymed, rhythmic prose — the Quran's distinctive cadence that is neither poetry nor ordinary prose, but a unique literary form.**

## Definition

Saj' (السجع) is the rhymed and cadenced prose that marks the Quran's style. The early Meccan surahs are remarkable for their short verses, rhythmic assonance, and parallel structures that build to a crescendo. Unlike poetry, saj' does not follow a fixed meter; its rhythm serves meaning, not decoration. The rhymes echo and reinforce major themes: life / strife / afterlife.
