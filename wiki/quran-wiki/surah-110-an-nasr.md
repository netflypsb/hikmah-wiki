# النصر — An-Nasr (110)

> An-Nasr — 3 verses, Medinan

## Overview

An-Nasr (النصر) is the 110th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Medinan** surah comprising **3 verses**, spanning **juz 30** (pages 603–603 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **1 rukus**.

## Tafsir Summary

### Ayah 1

**Ibn Kathir:** The Virtues of Surat An-Nasr It has been mentioned previously that it (Surat An-Nasr) is equivalent to one-fourth of the Qur'an and that Surat Az-Zalzalah is equivalent to one-fourth of the Qur'an. An-Nasa'i recorded from `Ubaydullah bin `Abdullah bin `Utbah that Ibn `Abbas said to him, "O Ibn `Utbah! Do you know the last Surah of the Qur'an that was revealed" He answered, "Yes, it was إِذَا …

**Muyassar:** إذا تمَّ لك -أيها الرسول- النصر على كفار قريش، وتم لك فتح «مكة» .
