# ق — Qaf (50)

> Qaf — 45 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Qaf (ق) is the 50th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **45 verses**, spanning **juz 26** (pages 518–520 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **3 rukus**.
