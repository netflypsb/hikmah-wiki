# الحاقة — Al-Haqqah (69)

> Al-Haqqah — 52 verses, Meccan

## Overview

Al-Haqqah (الحاقة) is the 69th surah of the Holy Qur'an. It is a **Meccan** surah comprising **52 verses**, spanning **juz 29** (pages 566–568 of the 604-page Madani mushaf), with **2 rukus**.

## Tafsir Summary

### Ayah 1

**Ibn Kathir:** Which was revealed in Makkah بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَـنِ الرَّحِيمِ In the Name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Warning concerning the Greatness of the Day of Judgement Al-Haqqah is one of the names of the Day of Judgement, because during it the promise and the threat will inevitably occur. Due to this, Allah has declared the greatness of this matter. So He says, وَمَآ أَدْرَاكَ …

**Muyassar:** القيامة الواقعة حقًّا التي يتحقق فيها الوعد والوعيد، ما القيامة الواقعة حقًّا في صفتها وحالها؟ وأي شيء أدراك -أيها الرسول- وعَرَّفك حقيقة القيامة، وصَوَّر لك هولها وشدتها؟
