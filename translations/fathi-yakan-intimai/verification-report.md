## 1. Summary of Findings

- **Qur’anic citations reviewed:** 81 unique verse references (some verses are cited more than once in the text).
- **Hadith citations reviewed:** 82 references to prophetic narrations.
- **Overall quality:** High. The vast majority of citations are accurate in sūrah name/number, verse existence, and wording.
- **Qur’an issues found:** 1 citation whose quoted text does not correspond to the cited verse numbers (Ṭā Hā 20:54–55).
- **Hadith issues found:** A small number of references are incomplete (missing book/hadith numbers), point to weak/graded narrations without flagging them, or contain minor attribution errors. None of these are fabricated; they are either well-known weak narrations or formatting issues.

**Status key:**

- **Verified** — sūrah/verse or collection/hadith checks out and the quoted text matches the standard translation/narration.
- **Needs attention** — the reference is technically valid but incomplete, uses a weak/ungraded narration, or has an attribution/formatting issue that should be clarified.
- **Error** — the verse/hadith numbers do not match the quoted text, or the collection attribution is wrong.
