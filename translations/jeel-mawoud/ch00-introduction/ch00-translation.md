### Introduction

All praise belongs to Allah; we praise Him, seek His help, seek His guidance, and seek His forgiveness. We take refuge in Allah from the evil of our souls and from the wickedness of our deeds. Whomever Allah guides none can lead astray, and whomever He leads astray none can guide. I bear witness that there is no god but Allah alone, without partner, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and His messenger. To proceed:

Allah has promised a group (*ta'ifah*, طائفة) of His believing servants that He will cause them to inherit the earth and grant them empowerment therein:

> "Indeed, the earth belongs to Allah. He causes to inherit it whom He wills of His servants. And the [best] outcome is for the righteous." (Quran, Al-A'raf 7:128)

Allah, Exalted and Majestic, did not specify a time or a place for the appearance of that victorious group; rather He specified conditions and attributes — whenever a generation from the generations, in any time and any place, fulfills them, then His promise, Glorified and Exalted is He, will be fulfilled for them:

> "Allah has promised those who have believed among you and done righteous deeds that He will surely grant them succession [to authority] upon the earth just as He granted it to those before them, and that He will surely establish for them [therein] their religion which He has preferred for them, and that He will surely substitute for them, after their fear, security — [for] they worship Me, not associating anything with Me. But whoever disbelieves after that — then those are the defiantly disobedient." (Quran, An-Nur 24:55)

## Page 6

However much the scholars strive in *ijtihad*, and however much the workers for Islam move here and there, this will not suffice to restore the glory of Islam anew — unless their movement and their striving include working to complete the attributes of the generation promised victory and empowerment (*al-Jil al-Maw'ud bi al-Nasr wa al-Tamkin*).

The promise of Allah does not fail:

> "And We have already written in the book [of Psalms] after the [previous] mention that the land [of Paradise] is inherited by My righteous servants." (Quran, Al-Anbiya 21:105)

This promise awaits those who fulfill the conditions of its invocation so that it may be realized.

History tells us that a generation of generations was able to fulfill the required conditions for the execution of the divine promise — the generation of the Companions (may Allah be pleased with them). Swift and generous was the fulfillment from Allah, Exalted and Majestic, to that generation: He caused them to inherit the earth, granted them empowerment in it, and they took possession of its lands within a few years:

> "Indeed, Allah has purchased from the believers their lives and their properties [in exchange] for that they will have Paradise. They fight in the cause of Allah, so they kill and are killed. [It is] a true promise [binding] upon Him in the Torah and the Gospel and the Qur'an. And who is truer to his covenant than Allah? So rejoice in your transaction which you have contracted. And it is that which is the great attainment." (Quran, At-Tawbah 9:111)

## Page 7

It is incumbent upon us — while we live through a critical stage of our ummah's history, with the pitch-dark night that envelops us, the dark tunnel we traverse, and the blood flowing from the suffering body of the ummah — it is incumbent upon us to work together, all of us, to bring about this generation promised victory and empowerment; to expend what is dear and what is cheap in the cause of its formation; and to reflect abundantly upon the generation of the Companions (may Allah be pleased with them) — how they were able to complete the requirements of divine victory and support — that we may follow in their footsteps and model ourselves upon them.

Around this subject — the generation promised victory and empowerment — revolves the discourse in these pages, in which we ask Allah to inspire us therein with rectitude and correctness, and to accept them from us by His grace and His favor.

> "Exalted are You; we have no knowledge except what You have taught us. Indeed, it is You who is the Knowing, the Wise." (Quran, Al-Baqarah 2:32)

Be like them if you are not of them, for imitating men is achievement.
