### Key Hadith Findings

- **74 verified / plausible** references.
- **10 flagged as suspect** for the reasons below.
- **Weak / unauthenticated sources**
  - **Al-Daylamī, *Musnad al-Firdaws*** (line 172): The hadith “Faith is not by wishful thinking…” is not from the Ṣaḥīḥ collections and is commonly classified as weak or fabricated (mawḍūʿ). It should be removed or explicitly flagged as weak.
  - **Al-Ṭabarānī, *al-Muʿjam al-Kabīr*** (line 160): Narrations from al-Ṭabarānī’s great compendium generally require independent grading; cited without a hadith number or authentication.
  - **Al-Mustadrak** references (lines 1528, 1686): Not one of the Ṣaḥīḥ Sittah; although al-Ḥākim is valuable, narrations need verification against al-Dhahabī’s grading and should not be cited as automatically authoritative.
- **Missing hadith numbers in Sahīh collections**
  - Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim, *Book of Faith* (line 148)
  - Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim, *Book on Government* (line 178)
  - Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī, *Book of Supplications* (line 184)
  - Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī, *Book of Heart-Softening* (line 1209)
  These are not errors, but they impede precise verification; full book-and-hadith numbers are preferred.
- **Likely wrong hadith number**
  - **Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī, *Book of Obedience*, Hadith 7137** (line 1718): The common canonical numbering of Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī does not reach 7137 (standard editions contain ~7008 ḥadīths). This number is probably a copy/paste or edition-specific error; the “hearing and obedience” narration is usually located in the Book of Obedience with a much lower number.
- **Same number reused for two different narrations**
  - **Jāmiʿ at-Tirmidhī, *Book of Asceticism*, Hadith 2349** is cited for two different texts: (a) the “merchandise of Allah is Paradise” hadith (line 114), which is plausible, and (b) the Bedouin martyrdom story (line 116), which is actually found in Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī / Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim, not Tirmidhī 2349. The second citation should be corrected.
