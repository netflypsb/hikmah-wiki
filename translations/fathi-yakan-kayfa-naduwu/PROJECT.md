- **Author**: Fathī Yakan (فتحي يكن, 1934–2009)

- **Edition**: 10th edition, 1417h/1996m (from OCR)

## Book Structure (4 Sections / مباحث)
1. **Introduction** (مقدمة)
2. **Section 1: The Obligation of Da'wah** (المبحث الأول: فرضية الدعوة)
   - Preachers on the Sidelines of Da'wah
   - The Obligation of Da'wah to Islam
3. **Section 2: How to Call to Islam** (المبحث الثاني: كيف ندعو إلى الإسلام)
   - The Method of Da'wah and Addressing People
   - Direct Individual Contact
   - Factors of the Preacher's Success
4. **Section 3: Topics Worth Raising** (المبحث الثالث: الموضوعات التي يحسن طرحها)
   - Critiquing the Current Reality
   - Critiquing Contemporary Trends (Capitalism, Communism)
   - Proving Islam as a Way of Life
   - Sources of the Islamic Methodology
   - Characteristics of the Islamic Methodology
   - Major Outlines of the Islamic Method
   - Basic Elements: Proving Allah's Existence, the Hereafter, Divine Decree
5. **Section 4: Organizational Movement is Necessary** (المبحث الرابع: التنظيم الحركي ضرورة)

## Translation Standards
- Quranic verses: Sahih International translation, cite as [Qur'an, Surah Name Number:Verse]
- Hadith: Full collection reference (e.g., [Sahih al-Bukhari, Book X, Hadith Y])
- Arabic terms: Original Arabic in parentheses on first occurrence
- Style: Faithful to original meaning, natural English prose, preserve author's argumentative structure
