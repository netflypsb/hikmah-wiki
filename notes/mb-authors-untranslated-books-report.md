## NOTES

1. **French intermediary translations** exist for Kawakibi's works (طبائع الاستبداد → "Les caractères de la despotisme", أم القرى → "La Mère des cités"). These could be used as reference for Arabic→English translation.

2. **Indonesian/Malay intermediary** available for:
   - Bayanuni: "Memahami Hakikat Hukum Islam" (1986)
   - Haraki movement critique (Malay, 2022) covering Yakan and others

3. **Mustafa Sibai "هكذا علمتني الحياة" (item 41730):** This item uses a compressed archive format, not direct PDF. The alfirdwsiy2018_gmail_7100 version may have a .bok format. An alternative copy exists at AAskZad as well. Verify format before use.

4. **AAskZad items** are from the Noor-Book.com / AskZad digital library — downloadable PDFs, typically image scans.

5. **BBib-Alex items** are from the Bibliotheca Alexandrina collection — scanned PDFs with text layers, downloadable.

6. **kutub_* items** (e.g., kutub_004, kutub_017) are from the "الكتب" (Books) collection — both image and text PDFs available.

7. **Image Container PDFs** require OCR (Tesseract Arabic) before machine translation. Text PDFs can be directly extracted.
